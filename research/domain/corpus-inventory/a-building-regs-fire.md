# Inventory A: Building Regulations and fire (England)

Checked 19 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

How I checked: the gov.uk Content API (`https://www.gov.uk/api/content/<path>`) for every gov.uk page, which gives attachments, change history and dates. I downloaded every current Approved Document PDF and read its cover, "takes effect" text and copyright page with `pdftotext`. legislation.gov.uk XML (`/data.xml`) for every piece of legislation. "Verified" means I fetched the URL myself on 19 Nov 2026. "Unverified" means I only saw it in a search snippet or could not open it.

## Licences (checked)

- **gov.uk pages**: footer says "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated" (seen on https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/structure-approved-document-a).
- **legislation.gov.uk**: "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0", footer "© Crown and database right" (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/help).
- **Approved Document PDFs**: the copyright page in each PDF says "© Crown Copyright <year> ... You may re-use the content (not including logos) free of charge ... under the terms of the Open Government Licence". This is true for B, D, E, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, O, P, Q, R, S, T, 7, the 2026 L and F, and the Manual.
  - **Exceptions**: the current **AD A** and **AD C** PDFs (2013 print) still carry the old wording: "If you wish to re-use, please apply for a Click-Use Licence". The gov.uk page footer still says OGL. That Click-Use was replaced by OGL is my understanding, not checked. Flag it for the legal review.
  - **Third-party images**: AD B 2026 lists picture credits, for example "© LABC", "© AHMM", "© Gas Safe Register" and a cover image © www.worldofmoose.com. **Ingest text only and strip images.**
  - **British Standard extracts** inside the ADs are not Crown material (rule already in `research/domain/methodology-sources.md`).

## How to fetch versions

### legislation.gov.uk (BR2010 and all SIs and Acts)

- **List of version dates**: `GET https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/contents/data.xml`. Read `<atom:link rel="http://purl.org/dc/terms/hasVersion" title="YYYY-MM-DD">`. On 19 Nov 2026 BR2010 had "made" plus 65 dates: 2010-10-01, 2011-07-15, 2012-01-16, 2012-03-21, 2012-04-06, 2013-01-09, 2013-01-27, 2013-01-28, 2013-02-06, 2013-04-06, 2013-04-19, 2013-04-20, 2013-06-03, 2013-07-09, 2013-10-01, 2013-11-14, 2014-04-06, 2014-04-30, 2014-07-31, 2014-10-01, 2015-04-18, 2015-07-31, 2015-10-01, 2015-12-31, 2016-01-01, 2016-04-06, 2016-04-08, 2016-05-01, 2016-05-09, 2016-06-17, 2016-12-08, 2017-10-01, 2018-01-15, 2018-02-28, 2018-04-01, 2018-04-02, 2018-06-08, 2018-11-01, 2018-12-21, 2019-01-01, 2020-01-13, 2020-12-31, 2022-06-15, 2022-11-23, 2022-12-01, 2022-12-26, 2023-03-29, 2023-06-05, 2023-07-04, 2023-09-05, 2023-10-01, 2024-04-06, 2024-04-25, 2024-10-01, 2024-11-04, 2024-12-17, 2025-01-01, 2025-07-01, 2025-10-07, 2025-12-20, 2026-07-01, 2026-07-20, 2026-10-01, 2026-11-06. History goes back to the start (1 Oct 2010). The Building Regulations 2000 it replaced is a separate SI (2000/2531), not checked.
- **Whole document at a date**: `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/<YYYY-MM-DD>/data.xml`. Any date works, not only version dates. Asking for 2024-01-01 returned the version with `dct:valid` 2023-10-01 (about 2.4 MB). One part only: `/uksi/2010/2214/schedule/1/2024-01-01/data.xml`.
- **Formats**: add `data.xml` (CLML), `data.akn` (Akoma Ntoso), `data.html`, `data.xht`, `data.htm`, `data.pdf`, `data.csv` (alternates listed in the XML).
- **Current**: `/uksi/2010/2214/data.xml` (`dct:valid` 2026-11-06). **Made**: `/uksi/2010/2214/made/data.xml`. **Prospective** (all future changes applied): `/prospective` in place of the date (documented at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/developer/uris).
- **Pending changes**: `<ukm:UnappliedEffect>` elements in the XML (33 on BR2010, from SI 2026/335, SI 2026/1094 and Welsh SI 2026/60). Feed: `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2010/2214/data.feed` (Atom).
- **Extent**: BR2010 is E+W. Welsh amendments (WSI) appear in the same document with extent markers. We must filter to England (`RestrictExtent`).
- **Coverage limit** (legislation.gov.uk/help): revised SIs are kept up to date for SIs made from 1 Jan 2014; older SIs only "selectively". BR2010 and FSO 2005 are in that selective set and are revised. Acts use the 1991-02-01 base date (Building Act 1984 versions start 1991-02-01).
- Simple amending SIs (2026/335, 2026/1094, 2018/1230) have no `/contents/data.xml`. That URL returns HTML. Use `/made/data.xml`.

### gov.uk (Approved Documents, guidance)

- **Metadata API, no key needed**: `https://www.gov.uk/api/content/government/publications/<slug>` returns `details.attachments` (title, PDF URL), `details.change_history` (dated notes, such as "Added 2026 amendments") and `public_updated_at`. Collection: `/api/content/government/collections/approved-documents` lists all 21 pages. Search: `https://www.gov.uk/api/search.json?q=...`.
- **Change detection**: poll `public_updated_at` and `change_history` weekly. Store PDF hashes, because files get replaced under new `/media/<id>/` URLs (AD B was replaced 14 Jan, 2 Mar, 11 Mar 2025 and 16 Sep 2026).
- **Older editions**:
  - AD B only: gov.uk "Historical documents relating to AD B" (back to the 2006 edition with 2010/2013 amendments).
  - Some amendment booklets stay on the live page, marked "No longer current".
  - The 2021 AD L/F stay on their pages beside the 2026 editions.
  - Everything else: National Archives UK Government Web Archive snapshots linked from each AD page (2014-12-02 Planning Portal snapshot for pre-2015 editions; 2015, 2016, 2019 and 2022 gov.uk snapshots). **Automated fetch is blocked**: curl and WebFetch both got HTTP 405 "Human Verification". We would need manual download or permission from TNA.
- **In-force dates**: read from (1) the "takes effect on" line on the PDF title page, (2) the circular, (3) the gov.uk page "Transitional arrangements". The standard old-edition test is: a building notice, initial notice or full plans given before the date, and work started (or "sufficiently progressed" for AD B) within a set time (12 months for many; 18 months for AD B 2026; 6 months for AD B 2029; 12 months to 24 Mar 2028 for L/F 2026).

## Gaps and open questions

1. **Click-Use wording in AD A and AD C PDFs.** The PDFs still say to apply for a Click-Use licence. The gov.uk footer says OGL. Get legal confirmation before ingesting A and C in full.
2. **National Archives web archive** blocks bots (HTTP 405). We cannot build AD history before 2019 for most ADs without manual download. Ask TNA, or download by hand.
3. **Some in-force dates not extracted**: AD A, C, D, E (latest amendments), AD G and M vol 2 2024 amendments (guess: 1 Oct 2024 with Part T), AD 7 2018 amendment, SI 2026/1094 start date, FSO 2005 start (1 Oct 2006 from memory). Need a second pass on the PDF "main changes" pages and circulars.
4. **Merged Approved Documents (Oct 2024) is out of date.** Do not use it as the source of truth.
5. **Manual to the Building Regulations (2020)** predates the BSA. Tag it as out of date, or leave it out.
6. **"Fire safety in purpose-built blocks of flats"**: licence unclear (LGA 2011 origin, no OGL line in the PDF) and content called "no longer comprehensive". A revised Home Office guide was promised for 2025. Not found.
7. **Probably missing, not yet checked**:
   - Building Regulations 2000 (SI 2000/2531) and its ADs, for old jobs. Low value.
   - Domestic Building Services Compliance Guide (mentioned in AD L change history; current status not checked).
   - LABC / NHBC / Planning Portal guides. Not OGL: link only.
