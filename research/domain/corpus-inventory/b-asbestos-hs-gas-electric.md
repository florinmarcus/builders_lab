Checked 19 Nov 2026. Not legal advice. Scope: documents for the shared regulations corpus of a construction assistant for small Romanian-run building and renovation firms in England, many doing council refurbishment, voids and repairs.

How "verified" was done: each URL was fetched on 19 Nov 2026 (curl through the session proxy, plus WebSearch "standard" for discovery). "Verified" means the page or PDF returned HTTP 200 and the edition and date facts were read on that page. "Unverified" means seen only in a search result or not opened.

## How to fetch versions

**Legislation (legislation.gov.uk)**
- Whole instrument, latest revised: `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/{type}/{year}/{number}/contents` (e.g. `uksi/2012/632`).
- As made (original): append `/made`, e.g. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/made .
- Point in time: append a date, e.g. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/regulation/20/2015-04-06 (tested, HTTP 200).
- List of versions: `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/{type}/{year}/{number}/data.xml`. The `hasVersion` links give every version date; `<dct:valid>` gives the start date of the latest version. Used for the dates in the table.
- Pending changes: `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/uksi/2012/632`. A "Not yet" in the last column means the change is not in the revised text yet. This is how we found SI 2026/1153 against CAR reg 20.
- **Important for CAR reg 20**: from 25 Nov 2026 the revised site may show the new reg 20 with an "in force 25 Nov 2027" note, or not yet. Store both texts with `valid_from`/`valid_to` and answer by job date.
- XML for ingestion: `/data.xml` (CLML) on any of the above URLs. Also `/data.pdf` for whole instruments.

**HSE publications**
- Landing page: `https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/{code}.htm` (L and HSG series) or `https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/{code}.htm` (INDG, CIS). Free PDF: `https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/{code}.pdf` (books) or `https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/{code}.pdf` (leaflets). Read "Date of publication", "Series code" (edition) and "Updated" from the landing page.
- HSE does not keep old editions on its site. Old editions: the Internet Archive availability API works, e.g. `http://archive.org/wayback/available?url=hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/l56.pdf&timestamp=2015`. Snapshots found (not opened): L143 Oct 2012, HSG210 Mar 2017, HSR25 Nov 2010, L56 Oct 2016, INDG223 Oct 2020. None for L144 (CDM 2007 ACoP).
- The UK Government Web Archive (webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk) returned a "Human Verification" page to our scripted fetch, so it cannot be crawled this way.
- Watch: HSE says it will update L143 and HSG248 for the reg 20 change. Re-check those two landing pages monthly.

**GOV.UK**
- Content API gives first published, last updated and change notes: `https://www.gov.uk/api/content/government/publications/{slug}`. Attachments are listed with direct asset URLs.
