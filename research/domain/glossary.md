# Romanian-English builder glossary (starter)

Checked 7 Nov 2026. Draft. Feeds query rewriting (see ADR 0001).

## How to read this

- **sourced**: backed by a source listed below.
- **unverified**: from general bilingual construction knowledge. Needs a check by a native-speaker UK tradesperson.
- **colloquial, unverified**: Romglish or site slang. We found no published source on UK Romanian site slang. Test these in Romanian UK groups or with builders.
- Romanian legal terms (diriginte de șantier, recepție, certificat de urbanism) should be checked against Romanian law (Law 10/1995, Law 50/1991). Not done.
- UK rules named in notes (VAT threshold, reverse charge, Part P, Gas Safe, Party Wall Act, permitted development) need a gov.uk check before use.

## Terms where the two systems do not match

These matter most for answers. A word-for-word translation would mislead.

- **autorizație de construire**: one permit in Romania. In the UK it is two separate things: planning permission and Building Regulations approval. Much work is permitted development (no planning) but still needs Building Regulations.
- **diriginte de șantier**: no UK equivalent. Compliance checks fall to the building control inspector.
- **deviz**: an itemised estimate. In the UK an "estimate" is not binding; a "quote" is a fixed price.
- **certificat de urbanism**: no exact equivalent. Closest: pre-application advice or a planning history check.
- **proces-verbal**: can mean a handover sign-off or meeting minutes.
