# Asbestos rules, surveys and registers (England)

Checked 7 Nov 2026. Not legal advice. Full report: `reports/Council refurbishment and asbestos.md`. Source notes: `research_notes/Council refurbishment and asbestos/asbestos_rules_and_surveys.md`.

Licence: HSE pages and HSG264 are under the Open Government Licence v3.0, so we can ingest them with attribution ([HSG264 page](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/books/hsg264.htm)). Council policies are council copyright: check each site before ingesting. Vendor pages and news are not OGL.

## Who has which duty

- **Reg 4, duty to manage.** Applies to non-domestic premises. HSE says this includes the common parts of blocks of flats: foyers, corridors, lifts, stairs, boiler houses, risers. It does not cover the inside of a flat. ([reg 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/regulation/4); [HSG264 para 10](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))
- The law asks for a "written plan" and for location and condition information to go to anyone liable to disturb asbestos. "Register" is HSE's word, not the regulation's. ([reg 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/regulation/4))
- **Inside flats**, councils still have duties as employers (HSWA 1974) and as CDM clients. Under CDM 2015 the client must give contractors project-specific asbestos information. General warnings are not enough. ([HSG264 paras 11–13](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))
- **Reg 5, the contractor.** An employer must not start work that may disturb asbestos until it has assessed whether asbestos is there, or has presumed it is and that it is not chrysotile alone. ([reg 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/632/regulation/5)) Wording paraphrased; check the exact text.
- Registers must be checked before all work that may disturb the fabric, "including simple and short duration work such as single hole-drilling". Information should reach contractors at tender or engagement, not on arrival. Printed copies "will usually be needed for contractors". ([HSG264 paras 146–149](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))
- Before major refurbishment, remove asbestos as far as reasonably practicable (reg 7, [HSG264 para 52](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf)).

## Work categories

| Category | Examples | Extra duties | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Licensed | Sprayed coatings, pipe lagging, loose fill, millboard, most AIB work | Licensed contractor | [HSE NNLW](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/licensing/notifiable-non-licensed-work.htm) |
| Notifiable non-licensed (NNLW) | Removing badly damaged asbestos cement, steaming off large areas of textured coating, short repair of a small piece of pipe insulation | Notify before work (online form only), medicals at least every 3 years, register of work and workers kept 40 years | [HSE NNLW](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/licensing/notifiable-non-licensed-work.htm) |
| Non-licensed | Removing intact AC roof sheets, painting sound AIB, drilling holes in textured coating | Controls, information, training | [HSE NNLW](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/licensing/notifiable-non-licensed-work.htm) |

The category depends on product, task and condition. So extraction must capture product type and condition, not just "asbestos yes/no".

## Training

- Three levels: awareness, non-licensable (including NNLW), licensable. Awareness is for trades that could disturb the fabric: electricians, plumbers, joiners, painters, plasterers, roofers. ([HSE training](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/training.htm))
- Awareness: no fixed annual refresher. Non-licensed and licensed: refresher every year. ([HSE training](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/training.htm))
- "Category A, B, C" are common labels from the L143 ACoP. **Unverified**: L143 not opened.

## Survey types (HSG264, 2nd ed. 2012, still current)

| | Management survey | Refurbishment and demolition (R&D) survey |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Normal use and foreseeable maintenance | Before any work that disturbs the fabric |
| Intrusion | Minor, simple tools | Fully intrusive and destructive |
| Scope | Whole premises or sample | Only the work area (whole building for demolition) |
| Presumption | Can be partly or fully presumed | Areas not accessed shown on plans and in text |
| Condition score | Yes | Usually no, unless removal is more than about 3 months away |

Source: [HSG264 paras 43–57](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf).

- "Refurbishment surveys will be required for all work which disturbs the fabric of the building in areas where the management survey has not been intrusive." ([para 50](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))
- **Housing stock** (paras 60–69): landlords group homes into archetypes by build date and design. Labels: "asbestos free" (generally built 2000 or later), "contain", "possibly contain". They survey a sample. Every unsurveyed home may contain any material found in its type. R&D surveys are needed only where the work happens, "eg cupboard, part of a room, kitchen/bathroom". ([HSG264](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))
- Homes of the same type still differ: builders' offcuts, unrecorded removal, tenant changes (para 61).
- **For us:** a "no asbestos found" result is valid only for the rooms and intrusion level surveyed. A kitchen refit needs a localised R&D survey for the kitchen.

## Report layout (HSG264)

- **Sections** (para 132): executive summary; introduction (scope); site and survey information; results (with material assessment); conclusions and actions; bulk analysis.
- **Executive summary** (para 134): locations with identified or presumed asbestos; specific areas not accessed; high-scoring items; actions and priorities.
- **Site and survey information** (para 136): surveying firm and address, surveyor names, client, premises address, report date, survey date, areas included and excluded, method, survey type, deviations, inaccessible areas with reasons.
- **Results** (paras 137–141): room by room. Rooms not accessed appear as presumed. Non-asbestos results go in a separate table.
- **Lab appendix** (para 143): lab name, UKAS number, method, results by sample ID, analysis dates, analyst signatures. Photos should be included (para 144).
- Source for all: [HSG264 PDF](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf).

## Register table (HSG264 Table 1)

Columns: Location | Product type | Extent | Accessibility | Condition | Surface treatment | Asbestos type | Sample no | Sampled / presumed / strongly presumed | Material assessment score | Priority score | Action.

Example rows from HSG264 ([p.43](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf)):

- "Store room 2, BC408 ceiling | AIB | Whole ceiling 120 m2 | Medium | Good | Painted one face only | Amosite | 1 | Sampled 4 samples | 5 | 12"
- "Plant room 2, BC416, pipe lagging | Pipe insulation | 24 linear metres | Easy | Good | Sealed and labelled | Crocidolite Amosite Chrysotile | 7 | Sampled 6 samples | 8 | 14 | remove during next campaign"

The floor plan (Figure 5, p.44) uses room codes and two shadings: "Contains asbestos" and "Not accessed, presumed to contain asbestos".

The register is simpler than the report: no site information or lab appendix. It is a "living document", updated on removal, new sampling or condition change (paras 146–147).

## Presumption and date cut-offs

- **Strong presumption:** looks like a known asbestos product, or similar material confirmed by the lab. **Default presumption:** not enough evidence, or not accessed. (para 37)
- Presumed items are scored as crocidolite unless strong evidence says otherwise (para 126).
- Presume asbestos-free only with strong evidence. Cut-offs: after 1985 for AIB and other amphibole products; after 1990 for textured coatings (banned 1988); after 1999 for chrysotile products (para 38).
- Source: [HSG264](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf).

## Material assessment score (HSG264 Appendix 4)

Add four factors. Total 2 to 12.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product type | – | Composites: vinyl tiles, asbestos cement, textured coatings, mastics, felts | AIB, millboard, textiles, gaskets, ropes, paper | Lagging, sprayed coatings, loose fill |
| Damage | Good | Low (scratches, broken edges) | Medium | High, delaminated, debris visible |
| Surface treatment | Composites | Enclosed sprays/lagging, painted AIB, AC sheets | Unsealed AIB, encapsulated lagging/sprays | Unsealed lagging/sprays |
| Asbestos type | – | Chrysotile | Amphiboles except crocidolite (e.g. amosite) | Crocidolite |

Bands: 10 or more high; 7–9 medium; 5–6 low; 4 or less very low. Non-asbestos items are not scored. ([HSG264 p.67](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg264.pdf))

Priority score: adds location, use, occupancy and maintenance frequency. Set by the dutyholder, not the surveyor alone (paras 128–129). The scheme is in HSG227, **not opened**. Vendor software follows this model ([PSI2000 manual](https://www.manula.com/manuals/psi2000/glossary-of-terms/1/en/topic/the-risk-assessment-algorithm), vendor, not OGL).

## How councils share asbestos information

- **Croydon** (policy reviewed March 2025): register in the housing system (NEC), "freely accessible to those likely to disturb the fabric". Contractors must hold awareness training, check the register, use a licensed subcontractor for asbestos work, "immediately suspend operations" on suspect material, return test and waste certificates within 10 working days. Unsurveyed pre-2000 homes: "the job must be postponed until a survey is carried out". ([Croydon policy](https://www.croydon.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-07/asbestos-management-policy-july-2025.pdf))
- **Tendring** (2025): sends survey copies or gives access to an online survey database; contractor reviews the register; permit-to-work form. ([Tendring policy](https://tdcdemocracy.tendringdc.gov.uk/documents/s73633/A5%20Appendix%20B%20-%20Asbestos%20Management%20Policy%202025.pdf))
- **Portsmouth, Medway**: register on shared IT system; contractors submit plans. **Unverified**, search summary only. ([Portsmouth](https://www.portsmouth.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/asbestos-policy-oct-2024-aa-accessible.pdf); [Medway](https://www.medway.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/4593/asbestos_management_plan.docx))

## Rules for the assistant (our inference)

- Never answer "no asbestos" when nothing was found. Say what was searched.
- Always show survey type, date, rooms covered and no-access areas.
- Flag when the work area is outside the survey scope, or the survey is only a management survey for intrusive work.
- Flag pre-2000 homes with no survey: Croydon says postpone the job.
- Tell the user to check the council's current register. Registers change and are often wrong.

Eval scenarios (inference): management survey only for a kitchen refit; bathroom R&D survey but work extends into the hallway; no-access loft or riser in the work area; pre-2000 flat with no survey; survey older than the last refurbishment.
