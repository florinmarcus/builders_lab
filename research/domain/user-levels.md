# User levels: documents, legal basis, pain points

Checked 7 Nov 2026. England/UK. Items marked **(unverified)** come from background knowledge or search snippets and need checking against gov.uk or HSE before use. Several gov.uk deep links returned 404 on the day, so some detail comes from accountants and law firms.

## Legal basis, with sources

### CIS
- Contractors must register if they pay subcontractors for construction work. Subcontractors need not register, but pay a higher deduction if they don't. Architecture, surveying, scaffold hire without labour, carpet fitting and material delivery are excluded. [gov.uk: What is CIS](https://www.gov.uk/what-is-the-construction-industry-scheme)
- Contractors must verify subbies, deduct, file monthly returns and keep records. Penalties apply. [gov.uk: CIS contractor duties](https://www.gov.uk/what-you-must-do-as-a-cis-contractor)
- Rates: 20% registered, 30% unregistered, 0% gross status. Return due by the 19th. Nil returns needed. £100 penalty one day late, £200 more after two months (secondary sources). [GoFile penalties](https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/construction-industry-scheme/penalties/), [GoFile deadlines](https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/construction-industry-scheme/filing-deadlines/)
- Deduction statements due by the 19th (unverified against HMRC). [GoFile](https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/construction-industry-scheme/filing-deadlines/)
- CIS does not apply when a homeowner pays a builder (unverified, consistent with gov.uk definition).

### VAT domestic reverse charge
- Applies when both sides are UK VAT-registered and the supply is reportable under CIS. Covers construction, repair, decorating, building services. Excludes architects and surveyors supplied alone. [gov.uk DRC](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-domestic-reverse-charge-for-building-and-construction-services)
- Not for homeowners. Invoice must say "reverse charge". End users should confirm in writing (secondary). [Pulse Accountants](https://pulse-accountants.co.uk/blog/vat-domestic-reverse-charge-a-guide), [BDO](https://www.bdo.co.uk/en-gb/insights/tax/vat-and-indirect-taxes/domestic-reverse-charge-vat-for-construction-services)

### Making Tax Digital
- MTD for Income Tax applies to CIS sole traders with qualifying income over £50,000 from 6 April 2026 (secondary). [Clear Books](https://www.clearbooks.co.uk/making-tax-digital-income-tax/builders/)
- Drop to £30,000 in April 2027 (unverified).

### CDM 2015 and domestic clients
- On homeowner jobs, client duties pass to the contractor (single contractor) or principal contractor (more than one). HSE says this "should involve little more than" normal H&S management. [HSE: domestic clients](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/domestic-clients.htm)
- A designer can take the client duties by written agreement. Without one, they pass to the principal contractor. [HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/domestic-clients.htm)
- HSE links the CIS80 construction phase plan guide from that page. [HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/domestic-clients.htm)
- F10 needed if work lasts more than 30 working days with more than 20 workers at once, or more than 500 person days. Sources differ on "working days" vs "days". [Designing Buildings](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/F10_notification), [HSE F10 help](https://extranet.hse.gov.uk/forms/F10/Form_Help.htm)
- Building Safety Act dutyholder duties for all building work since 2023 (unverified).

### Consumer law and the Construction Act
- 14-day cancellation for most off-premises consumer contracts. A quote given at home without agreeing the contract there counts as on-premises (FMB). [Business Companion](https://www.businesscompanion.info/en/quick-guides/consumer-contracts/consumer-contracts-off-premises-sales), [FMB PDF](https://fmb.org.uk/asset/859D7C64%2D1956%2D41D1%2D981B87FB93D9EC45)
- Construction Act payment and adjudication rules do not apply to residential occupiers (s.106). First appeal ruling: RBH Building Contractors v James [2026] EWCA Civ 511. Pay less notices should not be "traps". [BCLP](https://www.bclplaw.com/en-US/events-insights-news/court-of-appeal-guidance-on-pay-less-notices-and-first-ruling-on-the-residential-occupier-exception.html), [Ashfords](https://www.ashfords.co.uk/insights/articles/residential-occupiers-and-pay-less-notices-key-lessons-from-recent-construction-law-judgment)

### Building control and competent person schemes
- Schemes let installers self-certify electrics, windows and doors, heating, plumbing, ventilation, insulation, roofs and more. Gas work goes through Gas Safe. [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/competent-person-scheme-current-schemes-and-how-schemes-are-authorised)
- Notify within 30 days, homeowner certificate within about 8 weeks (unverified).
- Other work (extensions, lofts, structural) needs full plans or building notice and inspections (unverified).

### Accreditation
- TrustMark entry involves an insurance-backed guarantee and liability insurance. [FENSA/TrustMark](https://forms.fensa.org.uk/trustmark/apply-to-join)
- SSIP core criteria: H&S policy, organisation, risk assessments, method statements, training, accident reporting. [SSIP](https://ssip.org.uk/ssip-core-criteria/), [SafeContractor](https://www.safecontractor.com/contractor-accreditation/ssip/core-ssip-topics-covered)
- CHAS Elite adds the Common Assessment Standard, renewed yearly (unverified; CHAS pages returned 404).

## Admin time

All figures are soft. Most sponsors sell admin tools. No survey splits by level.

- HeyBRB 2026 (167 owner-operators, only 13% trades): 8 hours a week. 77% of trades do admin in evenings. Over a third take 20+ minutes per quote. 16% found AI too generic. [Electrical Times](https://www.electricaltimes.co.uk/uk-tradespeople-losing-ten-working-weeks-a-year-to-admin-they-could-automate-survey-finds/)
- Powered Now 2026: about 7 hours a week (unverified, snippet). [ECN](https://electricalcontractingnews.com/news/industry-news/powered-now-tradespeople-report-growing-business-pressures/)
- NOW Report June 2026: about 5h20m a week, 93% stressed (unverified, snippet). [Electrical Times](https://www.electricaltimes.co.uk/?p=36067)
- Merchant survey 2021 (700 builders): 62% struggle with paperwork. Two thirds do it evenings or weekends. Only 6% pay someone. [Builders Merchants Journal](https://buildersmerchantsjournal.net/paperwork-is-builders-biggest-stress-merchant-research-finds/)

## Pain points per level

| Level | Main pain points | Where AI fits best |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Design and build | CDM roles, building control, CHAS Elite renewals, payment notices, handover packs | Checklists, drafting policies and plans with sign-off, explaining clauses |
| 2. Small builder | CIS returns, unknown CDM client duties, verbal variations, late payment | Construction phase plan from a job description, written variations, CIS-aware invoices |
| 3. Sole trader | Quotes, invoices, getting paid, certificates, MTD | Voice-to-quote, invoice text, plain explanation of CIS and VAT rules |

Late payment: FMB/CIOB survey of 493 SME firms (early 2026). 57% paid within terms, 29% variable, 13% often late. [FMB](https://www.fmb.org.uk/resource/construction-smes-face-mounting-cost-pressures-and-insolvencies-despite-continuous-growth-report-warns.html)

Homeowner jobs have no Construction Act payment safety net. Written quotes, staged payments and signed variations are the main protection. [Ashfords](https://www.ashfords.co.uk/insights/articles/residential-occupiers-and-pay-less-notices-key-lessons-from-recent-construction-law-judgment)
