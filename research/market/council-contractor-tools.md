## Asbestos register software

- **TEAMS**: sold to asbestos consultancies and duty holders. Contractor portal shows "the latest reports and ACM details for the specific locations they work in". Enterprise pulls surveys back into the landlord's register. Survey Explorer by building, floor and room. Free iOS app; licence quote only. ([TEAMS portal](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/Portal); [Enterprise](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/enterprise); [solutions](https://teams-software.co.uk/asbestos-software-solutions); [client portal](https://teams-software.co.uk/client-portal); [compliance overview](https://teams-software.co.uk/client-portal/compliance-overview.htm); [app](https://apps.apple.com/lt/app/teams-portal/id1114454114))
- **Alpha Tracker** (Start Software): survey and register system. An article says most managed UK sites already hold live asbestos data in Alpha Tracker, TEAMS, Micad or similar. No price or contractor access found. ([FMJ](https://www.fmj.co.uk/?p=166964); [Alpha Tracker](https://www.agriculture-xprt.com/companies/alpha-tracker-start-software-59215))
- **Asbestos SMART**: contractor sign-in; register links can go into work orders. ([Asbestos SMART](https://d32j0cd29uv9q5.cloudfront.net/asbestos-smart-take-a-look-at-the-features-and-benefits))
- **Micad**: advises giving asbestos contractors admin rights to the module. ([Micad help](https://micad.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/5088429975197))
- **OpenAsbestos**: open standard for sharing asbestos data. ([UKATA](https://ukata.org.uk/news/openasbestos-open-standard-asbestos-data-sharing))
- Landlord holds the duty; under CDM 2015 the client gives asbestos information to the principal contractor. ([TEAMS blog](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/blog/do-housing-associations-have-to-remove-asbestos.htm); [Housing Plus Group draft](https://www.wrekin.com/Docs/Housing_Plus_Group_Asbestos_Management_Compliance_Policy_CONSULTATION_DRAFT.pdf))
- Registers are often wrong: one review found over 800 incorrect entries. ([Roofing Today](https://roofingtoday.co.uk/landlord-review-unearths-hundreds-of-incorrect-asbestos-records))
- Not researched: Shire, Opus/Codeshare, C365Cloud, Survey Pro.

## The gap

No product found lets a small contractor ask "where is the asbestos in this flat?" and get an answer from their own emails and attachments, grouped by property, citing the survey page and date. Not in Romanian, and not in English either.

What a new tool would need to beat Copilot on (**our inference, test with builders**):

1. Group by property across address variants ("Flat 12", "12 X House", job numbers).
2. Read PDF and scanned survey tables. Cite file, page and survey date.
3. Know survey type and scope. Never clear a room the survey did not cover.
4. Never say "no asbestos" when nothing was found.
5. Romanian answers, English source quotes kept as they are.
6. Works on a phone on site; maybe WhatsApp.
7. One flat price under about £16 per user per month (the Copilot benchmark).
8. No IT setup. Works with Gmail or Outlook.

## Risks

- Firms on Microsoft 365 may find Copilot good enough.
- Council contracts may forbid putting council data into a third-party tool (processor rules). See `domain/council-contracting.md`.
- A wrong "no asbestos" answer can hurt people.
- Data lives in landlord systems; the contractor only sees what is emailed or shown in a portal.
