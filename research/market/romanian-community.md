# Romanian community in UK construction

Checked 7 Nov 2026. No personal data about individuals.

## Short version

- Romanians are the largest foreign group in UK construction: about 5% nationally and about 19% in London.
- Rough estimate: 100,000 to 110,000 Romanian construction workers. Our own arithmetic, not a published figure.
- Most are probably self-employed under CIS. The only data is old (66% for Romanians and Bulgarians, 2014 to 2016).
- Numbers are falling. Romanians have the highest departure rate of any EU group.
- Channels are mostly informal: Facebook, WhatsApp, word of mouth, diaspora media. The Romanian Builders Network UK is the most direct channel.

## Estimate (our inference)

- UK construction workforce is about 2.1 to 2.2 million. 5% gives about 100,000 to 110,000 Romanians.
- With the old 66% self-employment rate, most would be CIS sole traders (level 3).
- Strongest in finishing and labour trades, London and the South East. Weaker in licensed trades (gas, electrics).
- 2026 numbers may be lower than 2023 because of net outflow.

## Language

- No study measures English ability among Romanian builders.
- A third of Romanian adults in England and Wales hold degrees. 39% work below their qualification level. [undelucram.ro](https://www.undelucram.ro/en/cumlucram/1-million-romanians-in-the-uk-what-the-workforce-numbers-mean-for-employees-and-recruiters-2435)
- Romanian is the second most spoken foreign language in London and Wales. [undelucram.ro](https://www.undelucram.ro/en/cumlucram/1-million-romanians-in-the-uk-what-the-workforce-numbers-mean-for-employees-and-recruiters-2435)
- Just over one in three site workers may not fully understand instructions or documents (all workers, not only Romanians; snippet only). [RICS](https://ww3.rics.org/uk/en/journals/built-environment-journal/site-safety-risks-language-gap.html)
- Principal contractors name language as a main safety barrier (Hull PhD). [Hull repository](https://hull-repository.worktribe.com/output/4221607/an-investigation-into-the-health-safety-and-environmental-management-of-migrant-workers-engaged-in-the-construction-industry-in-the-united-kingdon-uk)
- HSE case where training given only in English was one of the failings. [Enable Law](https://www.enablelaw.com/news-and-insights/protecting-migrant-workers-from-workplace-injury/)
- HSE and TUC made multilingual material because many migrants had limited English. [Personnel Today](https://www.personneltoday.com/?p=43839)
