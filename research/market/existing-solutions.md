# Existing solutions and gaps

Checked 7 Nov 2026. Prices are ex VAT unless stated. Most feature claims come from vendor pages, so treat them as vendor claims. "Unverified" means the claim comes from a secondary source or a search snippet, not the vendor's own page. Full report: `research/reports/Construction AI market and sources.md`.

## Short version

- No tool we found offers Romanian questions and answers over UK building rules, CDM or tax paperwork.
- Each single feature (rules Q&A, RAMS, quotes, AI receptionist) already exists in English. Each is cheap: free to about £50 a month.
- Big platforms are too expensive for small firms. Trades apps are the real competition at £25 to £45 per user per month.
- Language alone is not a moat. Any modern model can answer in Romanian.

## Gaps we could fill

1. **Romanian Q&A over UK rules.** Nobody markets it. Unverified whether vendors' models would answer in Romanian if asked.
2. **Small-firm duties.** Rules assistants target architects. Nobody focuses on CDM domestic-client duties, CIS and reverse-charge invoicing for small crews.
3. **Term accuracy.** Generic translation garbles construction terms. [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/publications/paper/6P1Q). A curated glossary is a real asset.
4. **Explain English documents in Romanian.** RAMS must stay in English for UK main contractors. Explaining or checking them in Romanian is open (our inference).
5. **Channel.** WhatsApp is used by a few English entrants. A Romanian WhatsApp assistant would be new. Heavy WhatsApp use in the community is likely but not sourced.

## Risks

- Competitors can add Romanian quickly.
- Builders may already use ChatGPT for this (not sourced).
- Price anchors are low: free to about £50 a month.
- Every rules tool carries a "not professional advice" disclaimer. We need one too.

## Open gaps in this research

- Prices not checked: BuildingRegs.app, Struck, ArchiChat, Regulex, Buildwell AI, RAMS PRO, DocGen, eDevize, Deviz 360.
- No independent accuracy tests of any tool.
- Capterra UK review pages returned 403.
- Polish or Bulgarian community tools (closest analogues) not researched.
