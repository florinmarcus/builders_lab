# Walk-through of existing AI tools

One group at a time: what they sell, and why a Romanian builder would or wouldn't choose them over us. Vendor pages checked 7 Nov 2026. Sources are the vendor URLs listed.

## 1. Regulation assistants

- **StructureGPT** (Atlasync AI Ltd, https://www.structuregpt.co.uk/): chat over England Approved Documents A–T, cited to section and page. Own uploads, diagrams, change alerts. For architects and engineers. Free (50 questions/month), £19 (500), £69 (2,000). England only. No languages, mobile or WhatsApp mentioned.
- **BuildingRegs.app** (Tidy Technology Ltd, https://www.buildingregs.app/pricing): same idea, wider sources (all four UK nations, CDM and HSE guidance, BS 7671, NHBC, planning). Free (15 questions/week), £145/month unlimited, enterprise on request. No languages stated.

**Against us:** free to start; built on LLMs, so they may already answer in Romanian (untested); BuildingRegs.app covers sources we can't ingest.
**For us:** aimed at designers, not builders; no CIS, invoicing or small-builder duties; no Romanian terms; office tools, not on-site.
**View:** on rules Q&A alone our edge is thin. Test them in Romanian (backlog item 2).

## 2. Safety paperwork generators (RAMS)

- **RAMSReady** (Bloor Engineering Ltd, https://ramsready.co.uk/): job description or pasted quote → full RAMS in about two minutes. Also COSHH, fire, inductions, toolbox talks, free RAMS checker. £29 one document, £49/month unlimited, £340/month for 10 seats, £299/month enterprise. User signs off; no compliance guarantee. No languages mentioned.
- **DocGen** (https://app.docgen.co.uk/): site video or short text → RAMS, construction phase plan, toolbox talk; chat to refine; QR inductions and sign-in on phones. Free for 2 RAMS/month; paid prices not on the main page. No languages mentioned.

**Against us:** specialists, cheap or free; the output must be in English anyway for main contractors, CHAS and inspectors.
**For us:** the builder must understand what they sign; the crew must understand the safety briefing.
**View (agreed with Florin, 7 Nov 2026):** don't compete on writing RAMS. Sit next to these tools: explain an English RAMS in Romanian, check understanding, give the crew a Romanian toolbox talk. Or point users to RAMSReady or DocGen.

## 3. AI quoting apps

- **QuickPrice** (https://getquickprice.com/): text, voice note, up to 5 photos or video → priced line items → branded PDF quote with one-tap accept → invoice. Learns your rates from 5 old quotes. Auto-chases quotes, tracks payments, lead form, diary. iPhone, Android, web. Free: 3 AI quotes per 30 days; paid prices not on the main page. VAT per line; no CIS. Material price source not explained. No languages or WhatsApp mentioned.
- **TradeQuoteAI** (https://tradequoteai.com/): type or speak line items → branded PDF quote and invoice, sent by WhatsApp or email. $12/month (20 quotes), $24 unlimited, $49 for 5 users with API. Prices in dollars. No CIS. No languages stated.
- **ToolsDownAI** (https://toolsdown.uk/): quoting over WhatsApp, waitlist per earlier search; site down (503) on 7 Nov 2026.
- Others seen only in search snippets: QuotePilot, Sleepless Tradesman (VAT and CIS), ToolBoxHQ, QuoteSmith.
- **Wickes Trade guide, summer 2026** (https://www.wickes.co.uk/trade/ai-tools-for-tradespeople-summer-26): recommends Tradify, Powered Now, VioTrade for quoting; Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent (CIS) for invoicing; and ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini for customer emails, RAMS, T&Cs. Cites 7–8 hours a week of admin.

**Against us:** very crowded, cheap, mobile apps already; a big merchant already tells trades which to use.
**For us:** none mentions Romanian. A Romanian builder could describe the job in Romanian (voice) and get a clear English quote for a UK client. Untested whether these apps already accept Romanian voice.
**View (proposed):** don't build a quoting app. Possible small angle: Romanian voice or text in, English quote text out, to paste into whichever quoting app they use.

## 4. Trades apps with AI added

From `research_notes/Construction AI market and sources/construction_trades_software.md` (vendor pricing pages checked 7 Nov 2026).

- **Tradify** (https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/pricing): jobs, scheduling, quotes, invoices, payments, Xero/Sage/QuickBooks sync, certificates. £34/£37/£44 per user per month. AI only on Plus: SmartRead (reads supplier bills), SmartWrite (job descriptions). Trustpilot 4.8 (625 reviews).
- **Powered Now** (https://www.powerednow.com/pricing): UK-built. £23.33–£33.33 per user per month. Only one advertising HMRC Making Tax Digital and VAT submission (page says including CIS) inside the app. AI writing and receipt scanning on Premium; AI receptionist add-on £29–£39 ("natural British voice").
- **Fergus** (https://fergus.com/uk/pricing/): from £39/£45 per month. Fergus Assistant (ask about your jobs and costs), Fergus Go (type or speak on site to update jobs), AI reading of supplier invoices from email.
- **ServiceM8** (https://www.servicem8.com/uk/pricing): priced by jobs (£0–£269/month), AI writing helper, AI office agent.
- **YourTradebase**: £29/month, no AI.
- None lists Romanian. Most leave CIS to Xero or QuickBooks.

**Against us:** they run the whole business (jobs, money, tax); the AI is being added inside the tool builders already pay for; switching cost is high once their data is in.
**For us:** their AI is small helpers in English; none explains rules or duties; none speaks Romanian.
**View (proposed):** don't compete with these. They are where the builder's data lives. Be a companion that works alongside them (Romanian explanations, English text to paste in), and maybe integrate later through their APIs (Tradify, Fergus and others connect to Xero, so APIs likely exist; not checked).

## 5. AI receptionists

- **Powered Now Receptionist** (https://powerednow.com/receptionist/): answers every call 24/7 on your existing number (call forwarding), takes the job details and texts them to your phone with a summary, recording and follow-up task. Texts callers who hang up. British voices to choose from. Urgent calls put through; family and regulars skip it. £29/month speech, £39 speech + text, £23.99 per extra teammate. No diary booking, quotes, WhatsApp or languages mentioned.
- Many small UK agencies sell the same (call answering, missed-call text-back, WhatsApp enquiries), about £39–£149/month (search snippets only, vendor claims; see research notes).

**Against us:** cheap, crowded, already inside a trades app; the caller is an English client, so Romanian isn't needed on that side.
**For us:** the builder's side could be Romanian: an English call summarised in Romanian, with a suggested English reply.
**View (proposed):** don't build a receptionist. At most, in direction B, a client message (call summary, email, WhatsApp) could land in the client folder with a Romanian summary and a draft English reply.
