# Walk-through of existing AI tools

One group at a time: what they sell, and why a Romanian builder would or wouldn't choose them over us. Vendor pages checked 7 Nov 2026. Sources are the vendor URLs listed.

## 1. Regulation assistants

- **StructureGPT** (Atlasync AI Ltd, https://www.structuregpt.co.uk/): chat over England Approved Documents A–T, cited to section and page. Own uploads, diagrams, change alerts. For architects and engineers. Free (50 questions/month), £19 (500), £69 (2,000). England only. No languages, mobile or WhatsApp mentioned.
- **BuildingRegs.app** (Tidy Technology Ltd, https://www.buildingregs.app/pricing): same idea, wider sources (all four UK nations, CDM and HSE guidance, BS 7671, NHBC, planning). Free (15 questions/week), £145/month unlimited, enterprise on request. No languages stated.

**Against us:** free to start; built on LLMs, so they may already answer in Romanian (untested); BuildingRegs.app covers sources we can't ingest.
**For us:** aimed at designers, not builders; no CIS, invoicing or small-builder duties; no Romanian terms; office tools, not on-site.
**View:** on rules Q&A alone our edge is thin. Test them in Romanian (backlog item 2).

## 2. Safety paperwork generators (RAMS)

- **RAMSReady** (Bloor Engineering Ltd, https://ramsready.co.uk/): job description or pasted quote → full RAMS in about two minutes. Also COSHH, fire, inductions, toolbox talks, free RAMS checker. £29 one document, £49/month unlimited, £340/month for 10 seats, £299/month enterprise. User signs off; no compliance guarantee. No languages mentioned.
- **DocGen** (https://app.docgen.co.uk/): site video or short text → RAMS, construction phase plan, toolbox talk; chat to refine; QR inductions and sign-in on phones. Free for 2 RAMS/month; paid prices not on the main page. No languages mentioned.

**Against us:** specialists, cheap or free; the output must be in English anyway for main contractors, CHAS and inspectors.
**For us:** the builder must understand what they sign; the crew must understand the safety briefing.
**View (agreed with Florin, 7 Nov 2026):** don't compete on writing RAMS. Sit next to these tools: explain an English RAMS in Romanian, check understanding, give the crew a Romanian toolbox talk. Or point users to RAMSReady or DocGen.
