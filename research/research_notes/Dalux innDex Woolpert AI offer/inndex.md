# innDex (UK construction site management software), as of 27 Nov 2026

Researched 27 Nov 2026. Most sources are innDex's own pages and press releases (vendor claims, not checked independently). Capterra and G2 pages returned 403 or had no prices, so pricing detail comes from search snippets only.

## What does innDex offer, who buys it, and how is it priced?

### Takeaway
innDex is a broad, modular site management platform (workforce, health and safety, assets, quality, sustainability) sold to tier 1 and large contractors (Turner, Morgan Sindall, VolkerFitzpatrick, Hill Group, McLaren, Siemens). Prices are not published; it is sold by demo and quote, per project per week or as enterprise plans. It is not aimed at 2 to 20 person firms.

### Cited Findings
- Modules listed on the home page: Workforce (Inductions, Competency Management, Fatigue Management, Time Management, Timesheets, Access Control, Briefings); Health and Safety (Accidents and Incidents, Observations, Inspections and Audits, Permits, Toolbox Talks, RAMS, Point of Work Risk Assessment); Assets and Logistics (Transport Management, Delivery Notes, Plant/Vehicles, Equipment, Temporary Works); Site Management (Progress Photos, Site Messaging, Daily Diaries, Allocation Sheets); Quality (Quality Checklists, Snagging); Sustainability (Emissions Tracking); Reports — [innDex home](https://inndex.co.uk/)
- Tagline "The Ultimate Construction Site Management Platform", "Built by construction for construction"; UK and US region versions; mobile apps on iOS and Android — [innDex home](https://inndex.co.uk/)

- Per-project per-week pricing and demo-only sales suggest a price point and set-up effort well beyond a 2 to 20 person firm (guess; no prices found).

## What AI features exist or are announced, and which LLM vendors?

### Takeaway
innDex's AI is early and narrow: AI Delivery Notes (photo to filled form, in pilot from Aug 2026) and a partnership with Navatech (Aug 2026) for AI in safety observation reporting. No AI RAMS writing, no AI assistant of its own, and no LLM vendor named.

### Cited Findings
- AI Delivery Notes (19 Aug 2026): "innDex reads the photo, pulls out the details, and fills in the form for you"; "We're trialling the updated AI Delivery Notes feature on pilot projects now, ahead of a wider rollout across innDex." No model, vendor or accuracy figures given — [innDex AI Delivery Notes](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/ai-delivery-notes)
- iOS app release notes: 4.0.42 (23 Oct) "AI-powered Delivery Notes"; 4.0.41 (19 Oct) adds an option to rate "AI feedback suggestions" and leave a comment (year not shown, presumably 2026) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/inndex/id1457696057)
- Navatech partnership (20 Aug 2026): brings "AI functionality to frontline construction teams to drive productivity and boost safety", focused on logging near misses, hazards and observations. No launch date, pricing, languages or LLM vendor stated. RAMS not mentioned — [innDex news](https://www.inndex.co.uk/en-us/news/inndex-and-navatech-partnership-to-bring-ai-to-frontline-safety-reporting)
- Navatech (third-party directory listings, not checked against Navatech's own site): UAE-based, founded 2019; AI safety assistant "nAI Hub"; guidance in 150+ languages with source document and page citations; used through its app, WhatsApp or Microsoft Teams by text, voice note or image; can generate toolbox talks, briefings and task guidance — [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/nAI-Hub/); [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/nai-hub)
- RAMS, inductions and home pages mention no AI features — [innDex RAMS](https://www.inndex.co.uk/rams); [innDex inductions](https://www.inndex.co.uk/inductions); [innDex home](https://inndex.co.uk/)

### Inferences
- The "AI feedback suggestions" in the app are probably the first sign of the Navatech work on observations (guess).
- Through Navatech, innDex could gain multilingual, voice and toolbox talk generation quickly. If that lands inside innDex, it covers part of what a small-firm assistant offers on site safety briefings, but for tier 1 sites.

### Gaps

## Customization: API, integrations, webhooks, white-labelling, custom forms

### Takeaway
innDex integrates with Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud and Asite, plus niche partners (ppac, Shoal, Bull Products, SmartSite, SiteHub). No public API documentation, developer portal or webhooks were found. Custom forms exist in a limited sense (embed your own assessment form).

### Cited Findings
- Procore: pre-enrolment and inductions push workforce details into the Procore directory; time and access data "sync attendance data to Procore"; RAMS and Toolbox Talks delivered in innDex and stored in Procore; safety observations feed into Procore — [innDex ecosystem post (8 Oct 2025)](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/building-a-smarter-construction-ecosystem-inndex-autodesk-procore-asite)
- Autodesk Construction Cloud: workforce data "syncs in real-time to Autodesk dashboards"; RAMS, toolbox talks, inspections, close calls and incidents "centralised across both platforms" — [innDex ecosystem post](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/building-a-smarter-construction-ecosystem-inndex-autodesk-procore-asite); confirmed by [Autodesk partner page](https://construction.autodesk.com/workflows/construction-software-integrations/inndex)
- Asite: innDex tools and Asite files sync automatically — [innDex ecosystem post](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/building-a-smarter-construction-ecosystem-inndex-autodesk-procore-asite)
- Other partnerships: ppac (workforce management, 24 Oct 2024), Shoal (4 Aug 2025), Bull Products (fire safety plus access control, 20 Jul 2026), SmartSite (hoist logistics, 21 Oct 2026); FORS validation in transport management (20 Nov 2025) — [innDex news](https://www.inndex.co.uk/en-us/news)
- RAMS: "Embed your existing assessment form" — [innDex RAMS](https://www.inndex.co.uk/rams)
- No API, developer docs or webhooks found in innDex's site, news or search results; the ecosystem post mentions no public API, Power BI, payroll or Microsoft integration — [innDex ecosystem post](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/building-a-smarter-construction-ecosystem-inndex-autodesk-procore-asite); [innDex news](https://www.inndex.co.uk/en-us/news)

### Inferences
- Integrations are partner-built, point-to-point links for big contractors' existing stacks. A small startup would likely need a commercial partnership to get data access (guess).

### Gaps
- Whether a private or customer API exists (likely, given the Procore and Autodesk links, but not documented publicly).

## Overlap, partnership options or threat for a Claude-based assistant for small UK builders

### Takeaway
Low direct threat: innDex sells to principal contractors on large sites and does not write RAMS or answer regulation questions. Overlap sits on RAMS and competency paperwork (innDex stores, approves and briefs RAMS; our assistant checks RAMS are filed and in date). The main touchpoint is that small subcontractors must satisfy innDex-run tier 1 sites, which hints at a "get your papers ready for the innDex induction" use case.

### Cited Findings
- innDex RAMS handles approval, briefing, sign-off and site access gating, not RAMS writing — [innDex RAMS](https://www.inndex.co.uk/rams)
- Workers build a reusable training and competency profile to submit for inductions — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/inndex/id1457696057)
- Customers are large contractors and housing groups — [innDex home](https://inndex.co.uk/)
- AI work so far is delivery note capture and observation reporting via Navatech — [innDex AI Delivery Notes](https://www.inndex.co.uk/news/ai-delivery-notes); [innDex Navatech](https://www.inndex.co.uk/en-us/news/inndex-and-navatech-partnership-to-bring-ai-to-frontline-safety-reporting)

### Inferences
- Threat level: low now, medium later if innDex plus Navatech add multilingual toolbox talks and RAMS help on site, and if innDex moves down-market. Nothing points to a down-market move yet (guess).
- Partnership: no public API or MCP, so any data link (for example pulling a subcontractor's induction status or site RAMS requirements) needs a commercial deal; realistic only once the startup has traction (guess).
- Product idea for the startup (guess): help a small firm assemble the pack a tier 1 site's innDex induction asks for (CSCS cards, right to work, RAMS, insurance), in Romanian, using the existing subcontractor-pack check.
- Our differentiators vs innDex: Romanian language, regulations library with citations, owner-level firm data (jobs, payments, emails), price for 2 to 20 staff.

### Gaps
- No evidence on how many small subcontractors use innDex, or whether innDex offers any subcontractor-side paid tier.
