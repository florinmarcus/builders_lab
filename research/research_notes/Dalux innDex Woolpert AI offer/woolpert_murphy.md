# Murphy Geospatial / Woolpert: offer, AI, digital twins (research as of 27 Nov 2026)

Scope note: murphygeospatial.com and murphygs.com could not be fetched (DNS error and redirect loop), so Murphy's own service pages were not read. Facts below come from Woolpert pages, press releases and trade press. Most AI claims are Woolpert's own marketing.

## 1. Acquisition, what the UK/Ireland business does now, clients, small builders

### Takeaway
Woolpert (US architecture, engineering and geospatial firm) announced the deal on 2 Feb 2024 and completed it on 27 Mar 2024. Murphy Geospatial is a large survey and digital construction contractor for infrastructure, airports, utilities and property, working for tier 1 contractors and public bodies. It is being folded into the Woolpert brand (full rebrand now targeted for autumn 2027). No evidence it serves small builders.

### Cited Findings
- 2 Feb 2024: Woolpert announced an agreement to acquire Murphy Geospatial, subject to regulatory approval — [Woolpert press release (plans)](https://woolpert.com/news/woolpert-announces-plans-to-acquire-ireland-headquartered-murphy-geospatial/); [GIM International](https://www.gim-international.com/content/news/woolpert-acquires-murphy-geospatial)
- 27 Mar 2024: acquisition completed; terms not disclosed. Murphy was private, family-owned, HQ Kilcullen, Ireland; nearly 400 staff, six offices in Ireland and the UK, founded 40+ years ago, 27,000+ projects. CEO Niall Murphy stayed on; traded as "Murphy Geospatial, a Woolpert Company" — [Woolpert press release, 27 Mar 2024](https://woolpert.com/news/woolpert-acquires-ireland-based-murphy-geospatial-a-leading-european-geospatial-solutions-firm/)
- Services listed at acquisition: survey, mobile and indoor mapping, asset monitoring, subsurface engineering, 3D digital twins, geospatial consultancy, aerial/UAV surveys, BIM, digital construction, topographical and property surveys, laser scanning, rail surveys, structural and environmental monitoring, subsurface investigation — [Woolpert press release, 27 Mar 2024](https://woolpert.com/news/woolpert-acquires-ireland-based-murphy-geospatial-a-leading-european-geospatial-solutions-firm/)
- Sectors named: infrastructure, manufacturing, property, natural environment, energy and utilities, plus construction — [same](https://woolpert.com/news/woolpert-acquires-ireland-based-murphy-geospatial-a-leading-european-geospatial-solutions-firm/)
- Earlier name: Murphy Surveys, renamed Murphy Geospatial in Oct 2020 — [GIM International, rebrand](https://www.gim-international.com/content/news/rebrand-marks-new-era-for-leading-geospatial-firm)
- Rebrand to Woolpert: Woolpert page says Murphy Geospatial will "fully rebrand as Woolpert by Autumn 2027"; an earlier version of the same page said autumn 2026 (target slipped a year). Murphy email addresses move to @woolpert.com from Friday 6 March (year not stated; 6 March was a Friday in 2026, so likely 2026 — guess). Service and data portals said to continue unchanged — [Woolpert, rebrand 2027](https://woolpert.com/murphy-geospatials-transformation-to-woolpert-in-autumn-2027/); [Woolpert, rebrand 2026 (older)](https://woolpert.com/murphy-geospatials-transformation-to-woolpert-in-autumn-2026/)
- Typical client work, Manchester Airport: Mace Construct (tier 1) hired Murphy for phase two (from Jan 2023) of Manchester Airport Transformation Programme. Services: control network, setting out, engineering survey, continuous laser scanning, scan-to-BIM, BIM-vs-design comparison, 360 photos, CAD deliverables, live dashboards, weekly reports, redlined drawings, handover docs. Claimed results: 10–12% fewer on-site errors, 18–20% lower claims and disputes cost, ~5% fewer extensions of time, 6,000+ issues found in six months. 2026 award finalist — [Woolpert case study](https://woolpert.com/case-studies/geospatial-services-enable-transformation-at-manchester-airport/)
- Aug 2025: partnership with Esri Ireland combining ArcGIS with Murphy's aerial, vehicle and terrestrial scanning for infrastructure on the island of Ireland (backdrop: Irish €100bn+ National Development Plan) — [Geospatial1 / Sensors and Systems, 22 Aug 2025](https://geospatial1.com/esri-ireland-and-murphy-geospatial-to-accelerate-rollout-of-vital-infrastructure-projects-2/)
- Murphy's property director describes scanning older buildings with LiDAR to make BIM as the start of a digital twin for retrofit and facilities management (article date not confirmed) — [PBC Today](https://www.pbctoday.co.uk/news/digital-construction-news/bim-news/making-retrofit-a-reality-with-geospatial-data/137683/)
- Second UK deal: Woolpert bought Bluesky International (Ashby-de-la-Zouch; UK's largest commercial aerial survey firm, 130+ staff; aerial photos, lidar, 3D models; Mapshop data shop; a main UK government supplier of aerial photos and height data), announced 13 May 2025 — [Geo Week News](https://www.geoweeknews.com/articles/woolpert-acquires-bluesky-international/); [PwC](https://www.pwc.co.uk/services/deals/recent-deals/pwc-corporate-finance-advises-on-sale-of-bluesky-to-woolpert.html)
- Woolpert: private, US (Dayton, Ohio), founded 1911, 75+ offices on five continents, ENR Top 50 design firm — [Woolpert press release, 27 Mar 2024](https://woolpert.com/news/woolpert-acquires-ireland-based-murphy-geospatial-a-leading-european-geospatial-solutions-firm/)

### Inferences
- Client base is large projects (airports, rail, utilities, government, tier 1 contractors). A 2–20 person builder is not their market. A small builder needing a measured survey would normally use a local surveyor (guess, based on project scale and sector list).
- Murphy is a services firm (people + scanners + CAD/BIM), not a software vendor.

### Gaps
- Murphy's own site was unreachable, so current UK office list, minimum job size and any small-job/residential offer are not confirmed.
- No current UK staff count after integration; no pricing.

## 2. Woolpert's digital twin offer: platforms, product or service

### Takeaway
It is a consulting and delivery service, not a product. The page says "digital twin services", "platform-agnostic", and names no software partners.

### Cited Findings

- Claims: combine real-time and historic data with "artificial intelligence, machine learning, advanced analytics, and spatial networking"; "scenario simulation, autonomous variable adjustment, and predictive analysis"; twins updated "without rescanning" — [same](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/digital-twins/)
- Industries: agriculture/environment, energy and utilities, facilities management, infrastructure, manufacturing, ports, smart cities. Case studies named: City of Melbourne (City Baths), Royal Łazienki Museum Warsaw (3D BIM), Singapore Land Authority (smart city) — [same](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/digital-twins/)
- Related Woolpert products/services listed: CAMACloud (Woolpert's property assessment software, for US local government — description is my background knowledge, not from the page), cloud solutions, data visualisation — [same](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/digital-twins/)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## 3. AI features and partnerships

### Takeaway
Woolpert's AI is mainly geospatial AI: automated lidar and imagery processing and feature extraction (via Allvision, 2023), plus Google Cloud AI consulting (Vertex AI, Gemini, Gemini Agent Builder) through Woolpert Digital Innovations. No public generative AI assistant product for construction.

### Cited Findings
- AI page: Woolpert "applies advanced geospatial AI"; AI "automates workflows such as lidar data processing and imaging analysis"; "AI-enhanced geospatial mapping"; Woolpert Digital Innovations, "a Google Cloud Premier Partner", uses "Vertex AI and Gemini" and Google Maps Platform; partnership with Rivers Agile on AI for geospatial data — [Woolpert AI page](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/artificial-intelligence/)
- 15 Nov 2023: Woolpert got exclusive rights to Allvision's AI for lidar and imagery processing; co-founders Aaron Morris and Ryan Frenz joined Woolpert; AI geospatial centre in Pittsburgh; goals include automated feature extraction and lidar-derived vector maps — [PR Newswire, 15 Nov 2023](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/woolpert-and-allvision-forge-strategic-agreement-to-enhance-geospatial-ai-capabilities-301989020.html)
- Aaron Morris is Global Director of AI; Woolpert reports an internal AI tool approval process and "over 30 AI tools developed securely" (search snippet from Woolpert pages; not verified on the page itself) — [Woolpert AI solution page](https://woolpert.com/solution/artificial-intelligence/)
- Early-2026 Woolpert Digital Innovations article on AI trends: focus on agents and Google Cloud ("Gemini Agent Builder", Vertex AI, BigQuery); its menu lists "Agentic Workplace Transformation". No mention of MCP, Claude, Anthropic or OpenAI — [Woolpert Digital Innovations, 2026 trends](https://innovations.woolpert.com/top-10-trends-shaping-ai-cloud-and-data-strategy-in-2026/)
- Feb 2026 trends report: 58% of Woolpert leaders polled expect AI to affect their markets in 2026 — [Woolpert PDF, Feb 2026](https://woolpert.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AEG-Themes-Shaping-The-Future.pdf)

### Inferences
- Strategic AI alliance is with Google Cloud, not Microsoft, AWS or Anthropic.
- Gen AI work appears to be consulting for clients on Google's stack, not a construction product.

### Gaps
- No evidence of AI-specific features in Murphy's UK survey delivery (for example automated scan-to-BIM) beyond general Woolpert claims.

## 4. Customization, APIs, data formats

### Takeaway
Custom work is the model: every project is scoped and delivered by staff. Deliverables named are point clouds, BIM models, CAD drawings, dashboards and reports. No public API or self-serve platform for the survey business found.

### Cited Findings
- Manchester deliverables: point clouds, BIM comparisons, CAD, 360 photos, live dashboards, weekly non-technical reports, redlined drawings, handover documents; no software or file formats named — [Woolpert case study](https://woolpert.com/case-studies/geospatial-services-enable-transformation-at-manchester-airport/)
- Digital twin page lists no file formats; says data collected once can be reused across applications and integrated with existing systems — [Woolpert digital twins](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/digital-twins/)
- Bluesky's Mapshop is an online shop for aerial imagery and height data — [Geo Week News](https://www.geoweeknews.com/articles/woolpert-acquires-bluesky-international/)
- Industry norm (other UK firms, not Murphy): measured building surveys come as DWG/RVT plans, elevations and sections, plus point clouds — [Sky Scan Surveys](https://skyscansurveys.co.uk/measured-building-surveys/)

### Inferences
- Murphy very likely delivers E57/RCP point clouds, Revit, IFC and DWG, as UK peers do. Guess: not confirmed from Murphy sources.

### Gaps
- No confirmed list of Murphy formats (IFC, E57), no public API docs.

## 5. Claude, Anthropic, MCP, OpenAI or other LLM integration; overlap with the startup

### Takeaway
No evidence of any Claude, Anthropic, MCP or OpenAI integration. The only LLM link is Google Gemini via Woolpert's Google Cloud consulting arm. Overlap with a Claude assistant for small Romanian-run UK builders is very low; not a threat and not a natural partner.

### Cited Findings
- Search for Woolpert with MCP, Anthropic, Claude or OpenAI returned nothing about Woolpert; other geospatial vendors (CARTO, Precisely, Woosmap) do ship MCP servers — [CARTO MCP](https://carto.com/blog/carto-mcp-server-turn-your-ai-agents-into-geospatial-experts/); [Precisely MCP](https://www.precisely.com/press-release/precisely-accelerates-location-aware-ai-with-model-context-protocol)
- Woolpert's 2026 AI content names only Google Gemini / Vertex AI — [Woolpert Digital Innovations](https://innovations.woolpert.com/top-10-trends-shaping-ai-cloud-and-data-strategy-in-2026/); [Woolpert AI page](https://woolpert.com/solutions/data-and-technology/artificial-intelligence/)

### Inferences
- Overlap: very low. Woolpert/Murphy sell high-end survey and twin services to tier 1, airports, utilities and governments. The startup sells a phone assistant for job admin, payments, regulations to 2–20 person builders. Different buyer, different size, different problem.
- Threat: negligible in the near term. Guess.
- Partnership: unlikely to matter to Woolpert at this scale. A small builder rarely buys from them.
- Survey outputs feeding quoting: possible in principle. A measured survey PDF or DWG (floor plans, areas, heights) could be read by Claude to estimate quantities (wall area, floor area) for a quote. But the source would be local measured-survey firms, not Murphy. Point clouds and BIM are too heavy for small builders. A practical path is "upload the surveyor's PDF plans, Claude reads dimensions" as a later feature, with clear checks. All guess.
- Bluesky aerial data (Mapshop) could in theory give roof or site context for quotes, but cost and licensing are unknown. Guess.

### Gaps
- No public statement from Woolpert on MCP or third-party AI assistant integrations.
- No Murphy pricing or minimum job size to confirm they ignore small builders.
