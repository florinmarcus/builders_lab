# Dalux (Copenhagen, construction software) as of 27 Nov 2026

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Some Dalux help pages (support.dalux.com) returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, so facts from
them come from search-engine extracts of those pages. They are marked "(search extract)". dalux.com root and
integrations pages were fetched directly on 27 Nov 2026.

## 1. Products, customers, firm size, UK presence and pricing

### Takeaway
Dalux is a large BIM-centred suite (Box, Tender, BIM Viewer, Field, InfraField, Field Basic, FM, Handover) used mostly by
mid-size and large contractors and owners. It has a UK company on the G-Cloud framework. Two products are free (BIM
Viewer, Field Basic). Paid products are quote-only, priced on project construction value or per user.

### Cited Findings
- Product groups on dalux.com: Information Management (Dalux Box, Tender, BIM Viewer, free); On-site Management (Dalux
  Field, Dalux InfraField, Field Basic, free); Facility Management (Dalux FM, Handover). — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/)
- Claims "Trusted by 1,700,000 Users". Customer logos include Strabag, Vinci, Bouygues, WSP; UK quote from Hill Group's
  Group BIM Manager. — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/)
- Integrations page shows customer logos Strabag, Hines, WSP, Vinci, Sisk, Kajima. — [Dalux integrations](https://www.dalux.com/solutions/dalux-integrations/)
- Field Basic: free snagging tool on drawings and BIM, free invitations for project members; BIM Viewer free for desktop
  and mobile; both allow free projects. — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/)
- Headquartered in Copenhagen. — [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/dalux/5a1da3502300005c009569c4)
- Staff: 570 employees and 41 offices in company report 2023/24 (per Dalux LinkedIn post, via search); 902 employees
  (Sep 2025) per [Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/dalux/employees/); about 878 (Apr 2026) per
  [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/dalux/5a1da3502300005c009569c4). Third-party estimates, not audited.
- Countries: claims of 90+ ([Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/organization/dalux)) to 150+ (recent Dalux material, via
  search). The site selector lists about 28 countries. — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/)
- Revenue: third-party estimates conflict by 10x or more (ZoomInfo $18.6M; Prospeo about $6.1M; LeadIQ $100M-$250M).
  Not reliable. — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/dalux/20433953); [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/dalux-revenue);
  [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/dalux/5a1da3502300005c009569c4)
- Firm size of reviewers: G2 Dalux reviewers 43.8% mid-market, 32.5% enterprise, 23.7% small business (Fieldwire: 54.2%

- UK: DALUX LIMITED lists "Dalux Box Pro" on G-Cloud 15 as an ISO 19650 Common Data Environment built around BIM (shared
  and published areas, approval workflows, naming rules, clash checks, IFC/RVT/DGN federation, plugins for Revit,
  Navisworks, Tekla, ArchiCAD). Data stored in UK or EEA on AWS; customer cannot choose. ISO 27001 dated 10 Jun 2025.
  Documents dated 29-30 Jan 2026. — [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/878783968162363)

- Small firms most likely meet Dalux as subcontractors invited free into a main contractor's Field or Box project

## 2. AI features today, beta and announced; LLM vendor

### Takeaway
Dalux's one confirmed AI feature is an "AI Assistant" in Dalux Box that summarises and answers questions about a single
text document (PDF or .docx). It began a phased roll-out with release 2026.1 (late Feb 2026). Dalux does not name its
model vendor.

### Cited Findings
- Release 2026.1 rolled out 21-28 Feb 2026 by region; the AI Assistant is rolled out gradually, "availability will expand
  over time". — [Build release notes 2026.1](https://support.dalux.com/hc/cs/articles/23976440268572-Build-release-notes-2026-1);
  [AI Assistant help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19728327500060) (search extract)
- What it does: preset actions, or ask it to find information in multi-page documents. Works only on text-based files in
  Dalux Box (PDF and .docx). Image-heavy or complex PDFs and drawings may not work. Answers from the open document only and
  says when it cannot find an answer. Does not use other projects, other documents or other versions of the file. —
  [AI Assistant help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19728327500060-AI-Assistant) (search extract)
- Data: "processed within Dalux's controlled cloud environment and is not used to train public AI models"; follows existing
  Dalux permissions. — [AI Assistant help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19728327500060) (search extract)
- Dalux homepage and integrations page mention no AI features (fetched 27 Nov 2026). — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/);
  [Dalux integrations](https://www.dalux.com/solutions/dalux-integrations/)
- G-Cloud 15 Box Pro listing (Jan 2026) mentions no AI features. — [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/878783968162363)
- Dalux ran "Sneak peek" webinars; the pages give no product detail. — [Dalux sneak peek](https://www.dalux.com/nl/?p=348008)
- No source names the LLM vendor (OpenAI, Azure, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock or other). The sub-processor list shows only AWS
  and Zendesk. — [Dalux sub processors](https://dalux.com/it/subprocessors)

### Inferences
- Because hosting is AWS and no AI sub-processor is listed, the model may run through AWS (for example Bedrock) inside
  Dalux's AWS account. This is a guess, not confirmed.
- The assistant is single-document Q&A, not cross-project search, not drawing or photo AI, and not regulations advice.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of defect-photo AI, drawing recognition AI, auto-tagging, or cross-document search. Release notes
  2026.2-2026.5 and FM release notes could not be read in full (HTTP 403).
- Whether the AI Assistant costs extra is not stated.
- Model vendor unknown; would need to ask Dalux.

## 3. Customization: API, access terms, integrations, webhooks, white-label, forms

### Takeaway
Dalux has REST APIs (Build API covering Field and Box; FM API) documented on SwaggerHub. Access needs a company licence
and a key from the Dalux Customer Success Manager. The API is partly read-only (no creating tasks or forms), has no
webhooks and no sandbox. Named third-party integrations are mostly BIM design plugins and open file formats.

### Cited Findings
- API lets external software read and change Dalux data with GET, POST and PATCH. Needs a company licence; contact your
  Customer Success Manager to get a key. Specs on SwaggerHub (search "owner:Dalux"). — [Dalux API help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/articles/9544314902556) (search extract)
- "API identities" replace older company API keys; old keys set to expire after 28 Feb 2026. FM API supports identities
  from v2.0.0. — [API identities, Build](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/20892369915292);
  [API identities, FM](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19340181684892) (search extract)
- Key sent in an X-API-KEY header. — [Jentic](https://jentic.com/apis/dalux.com/dalux.md); [Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/baxsbdeuy8)
- G-Cloud: OpenAPI (Swagger) docs; no sandbox or test environment; "customisation" not available; dashboard data
  available through the API for BI tools; IFC, BCF, COBie, CSV import and export; SSO and MFA. — [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/878783968162363)
- Per the community MCP README: as of Build API 4.15 you cannot create or update tasks and forms (Field data is
  read-only), manage users, use webhooks (poll incremental change feeds instead), or query BIM geometry. —
  [mcp-server-dalux](https://github.com/borgels/mcp-server-dalux) (search extract of README)
- Dalux integrations page: links between Dalux products (Box to Tender, Handover, Field, InfraField; Handover to FM;
  "Project Connect" folder sync), plugins for design software, API for custom workflows and dashboards. No Autodesk,
  Microsoft, Procore, ERP or webhook named. — [Dalux integrations](https://www.dalux.com/solutions/dalux-integrations/)
- Third parties: Unified.to hosted Dalux connector (Beta; tasks, HR and directory). — [Unified.to](https://unified.to/integrations/dalux).
  Community GitHub projects: a Power Platform connector, an unofficial Python/JS Build API wrapper (updated 25 Nov 2026),
  FM API scripts. — [LogiqApps connector](https://github.com/LogiqApps-AS/dalux-sync-power-platform-connector);
  [bruadam/dalux-build](https://github.com/bruadam/dalux-build)

### Inferences
- An outside product cannot write snags or forms into Dalux Field through the API today; it can read them and upload
  files to Box.
- No webhooks means any sync must poll.
- No evidence of white-labelling or a public app marketplace.

### Gaps
- API price (if any beyond the licence) and rate limits not published.
- Custom forms and checklist templates exist in Field (named in the MCP README) but no Dalux page on form builders was read.

## 4. Claude, Anthropic, MCP, OpenAI or other LLM integrations

### Takeaway
No official Dalux integration with Claude, Anthropic, MCP, ChatGPT or OpenAI was found. One community MCP server for the
Dalux Build API exists on GitHub.

### Cited Findings
- `borgels/mcp-server-dalux`: unofficial MCP server for the Dalux Build API (Field tasks, safety issues, forms and
  checklists, inspection and test plans; Box file areas, folders, files, version sets). Read tools by default; writes
  opt-in with `DALUX_ENABLE_WRITES=true` (chunked file upload, create/update companies and projects). Runs over stdio or
  streamable HTTP; Docker image on ghcr.io. Needs only the Dalux API key. — [GitHub](https://github.com/borgels/mcp-server-dalux)
  (search extract; direct fetch timed out); [Glama listing](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/baxsbdeuy8)
- No Dalux-made MCP server appeared in search; results were the community one only. — [GitHub](https://github.com/borgels/mcp-server-dalux)
- No source names an LLM vendor behind the Dalux AI Assistant. — [AI Assistant help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19728327500060) (search extract)

### Inferences
- A Claude user whose firm has a paid Dalux company licence could connect Claude to Dalux today with the community MCP
  server, read-only for Field data.

### Gaps
- Licence, star count, last commit date and security of the community MCP server not checked (GitHub API access blocked
  in this session).
- Dalux LinkedIn posts and press releases on AI plans not checked.

## 5. Overlap, partnership or threat for a Claude-based assistant for small UK firms

### Takeaway
Low direct threat today. Dalux's AI is narrow (one document at a time inside Box) and its buyers are larger, BIM-led
firms. The main overlap is document Q&A and snagging/handover records. A read-only Dalux connector could be useful for
small firms that work as subcontractors on Dalux projects.

### Cited Findings
- AI limited to single-document Q&A in Box. — [AI Assistant help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/en-us/articles/19728327500060) (search extract)
- Free Field Basic and free project invitations reach small subcontractors. — [dalux.com](https://www.dalux.com/)
- API needs a company licence and CSM approval; Field data read-only. — [Dalux API help](https://support.dalux.com/hc/articles/9544314902556) (search extract);
  [mcp-server-dalux](https://github.com/borgels/mcp-server-dalux) (search extract)
- Small businesses are about a quarter of Dalux reviewers on G2. — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/dalux-vs-fieldwire-by-hilti)

### Inferences (all guesses)
- Threat: if Dalux widens its assistant to cross-project search plus snag and handover data, it would cover "what is
  missing before handover" for firms already on Dalux. It shows no sign of UK regulations advice, Romanian language,
  email triage or payment-notice dates.
- Partnership: no partner programme or app marketplace was found, so a formal partnership looks unlikely at this firm
  size. A practical path is a read-only "Dalux import" for main-contractor snag lists sent to a small sub, using the API
  if the main contractor grants access, or using Dalux PDF/Excel exports.
- Small firms invited free into Dalux projects probably cannot get an API key themselves (they hold no company licence).

### Gaps
- No UK small-firm Dalux adoption figures found.
- No data on Dalux use by Romanian-run UK firms.
