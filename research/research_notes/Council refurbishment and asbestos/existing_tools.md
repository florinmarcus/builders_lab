# Existing tools for small social housing contractors: property documents and asbestos information (checked 7 Nov 2026)

Scope: what small (1-10 person) contractors refurbishing council/social housing flats in England use, or could use, to find property documents and asbestos information, and what it costs. All prices in GBP unless marked, checked 7 Nov 2026. Many prices come from third-party review sites, not vendors: these are marked. Vendor marketing claims are marked as such.

## 1. Contractor-side job management tools (Kirona, Totalmobile, Joblogic, BigChange, simPRO, Commusoft, Plentific, etc.)

### Takeaway
Job management tools exist at two levels. Kirona and Totalmobile sell to landlords and large contractors (enterprise deals, no public prices). Joblogic, Commusoft, BigChange and simPRO sell to trade contractors at roughly £45-£105 per user per month (third-party figures; most vendors now hide prices). None of the sources found shows any of them offering "search all the council's emails and attachments for this property". Asbestos data in these tools is a field or attachment on a site/job, if the landlord sends it.

### Cited Findings
- Kirona: about 80% of its revenue comes from social housing; its Job Manager customers are landlords (Salix Homes, Anchor, Riverside) and a large contractor (Cruden). Anchor used Job Manager for recurring compliance checks (Legionella, communal taps). No result linked Kirona to asbestos-specific features. — [Housing Technology, Kirona vendor page](https://www.housing-technology.com/vendors/kirona/); [Salix case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/kirona/case-study/salix-homes-implements-mobile-working-solution-to-save-5000-per-worker); [Anchor case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/kirona/case-study/anchor-chooses-kirona-for-online-scheduling-and-workforce-management); [Riverside case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/kirona/case-study/kirona-enable-riverside-to-improve-customer-service)
- Kirona at Riverside held "special instructions for each repair" in the scheduling system (vendor case study). — [Riverside case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/kirona/case-study/kirona-enable-riverside-to-improve-customer-service)
- Joblogic: Standard tier listed at £45 per user per month billed annually (third-party, July 2026), another November 2026 comparison says £49; Premium and Enterprise are quote-only; per-user pricing reportedly scales up to 50 users; free trial, no free plan. Sources disagree (older listings say £35). — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/134632/joblogic); [GetApp UK](https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/103963/joblogic); [Whito price comparison](https://whito.co.uk/tools/job-management-software-uk-trades-cost/); [Facilio blog (competitor)](https://facilio.com/blog/joblogic-pricing/)
- No Joblogic page was found describing an asbestos register or asbestos field per site. — (search returned no Joblogic result; gap)
- A comparable maintenance platform, Access Maintain, notifies contractors when asbestos is present, shows the asbestos report on engineers' mobile accounts and reports sites missing asbestos information. This is the pattern: the asbestos report is an attachment pushed by the client. — [Access Maintain help](https://help-accessmaintain.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/12889794-managing-asbestos-reports)
- Commusoft: no public price; 12-month contracts; one review site says £55-£105 per user per month + VAT with a 3-user minimum; Capterra lists from £59 per user. (Third-party, unverified.) — [Capterra UK, Commusoft](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/189894/commusoft); [Kaeltripton review](https://www.kaeltripton.com/commusoft-uk/)
- BigChange: now quote-only; third-party listings put job management around £79.95 per licence per month and vehicle tracking from £14.95; another says £55-£75 per user per month plus telematics. (Unverified.) — [Capterra UK, BigChange](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/149479/bigchange); [Whito](https://whito.co.uk/tools/job-management-software-uk-trades-cost/)
- simPRO: quote only; a competitor's page claims £700-£1,500 a month (self-interested, unverified). — [Whito](https://whito.co.uk/tools/job-management-software-uk-trades-cost/)
- A November 2026 tracker found Commusoft, Simpro, BigChange, Klipboard and Workiz show no price on their UK pricing pages. — [Whito](https://whito.co.uk/tools/job-management-software-uk-trades-cost/)
- Plentific: landlord-side repairs platform built with G15 housing associations; landlords can send work orders to preferred contractors, own staff or Plentific's marketplace; contractors use a Field Service App to manage jobs and message property managers; "Open Framework" claims verified contractors get access to 100+ housing providers (vendor claim). No contractor price found. — [Housing Technology](https://www.housing-technology.com/plentific-expands-online-contractor-platform); [Plentific Open Framework](https://www.plentific.com/open-framework-powered-by-plentific/); [Plentific social housing](https://plentific.com/sectors/social-housing); [Plentific field service](https://www.plentific.com/field-service-management/)

### Inferences
- For a 3-5 person firm, a trade job management tool costs roughly £150-£500 a month (based on the third-party per-user figures above). That is a lot for a firm that mainly needs to find documents.
- Landlord systems (Kirona, Plentific, Totalmobile, housing management systems) are paid for by the landlord. The contractor sees only what the landlord pushes per job. Nothing found suggests the contractor can search across the landlord's whole history for a property, or across their own emails.
- Small Romanian-run subcontractors probably sit below the tier that uses these tools at all and run on email, WhatsApp and paper (guess, not verified).

### Gaps
- Totalmobile, Sypro and Klipboard: no prices or contractor-size data gathered in this pass.
- Contractor portals of NEC Housing, Civica (Cx) and MRI: not researched; unknown whether they show asbestos data to contractors per property.
- No data found on how many 1-10 person firms use any of these tools.
- No vendor confirmed Romanian-language interfaces (not searched directly).

## 2. Asbestos register and compliance software (TEAMS, Alpha Tracker, Shire, Micad, Asbestos SMART, etc.)

### Takeaway
Asbestos register software is bought by landlords (duty holders) and asbestos consultancies/surveyors. Several products do give contractors a login or link to see the register for the property they work in, but only if the landlord sets it up. Prices are not published.

### Cited Findings
- TEAMS is sold to asbestos consultancies and duty holders. Its portal lets a contractor see the latest reports and asbestos-containing material (ACM) details for the specific locations they work in on site. TEAMS Enterprise pulls contractor-finalised surveys and item data back into the landlord's central register. The TEAMS Portal iOS app is free; software licence prices are not published (quote or demo only). — [TEAMS portal](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/Portal); [TEAMS Enterprise](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/enterprise); [TEAMS solutions](https://teams-software.co.uk/asbestos-software-solutions); [TEAMS Portal app](https://apps.apple.com/lt/app/teams-portal/id1114454114)
- TEAMS client portal has a Survey Explorer to navigate reports by building, floor and room, and an "Assets by Risk" view. — [TEAMS client portal](https://teams-software.co.uk/client-portal); [Compliance Overview](https://teams-software.co.uk/client-portal/compliance-overview.htm)
- Alpha Tracker is an asbestos surveying and register system from Start Software. An industry article says most managed UK sites already have live asbestos data in Alpha Tracker, TEAMS, Micad or similar. No contractor-access or price details found. — [FMJ article](https://www.fmj.co.uk/?p=166964); [Alpha Tracker / Start Software](https://www.agriculture-xprt.com/companies/alpha-tracker-start-software-59215)
- Asbestos SMART offers a contractor sign-in; links to registers can be put into work orders and instructions to contractors. — [Asbestos SMART features](https://d32j0cd29uv9q5.cloudfront.net/asbestos-smart-take-a-look-at-the-features-and-benefits)
- Micad advises that asbestos contractors be given Administrator rights to the asbestos module. — [Micad help](https://micad.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/5088429975197)
- UKATA reports on "OpenAsbestos", an open standard for sharing asbestos data. — [UKATA](https://ukata.org.uk/news/openasbestos-open-standard-asbestos-data-sharing)
- Landlords hold the legal duty to identify, record and manage asbestos (Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012). Under CDM 2015, the landlord as client provides existing asbestos information to the principal contractor (Housing Plus Group policy). — [TEAMS blog](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/blog/do-housing-associations-have-to-remove-asbestos.htm); [Housing Plus Group policy draft](https://www.wrekin.com/Docs/Housing_Plus_Group_Asbestos_Management_Compliance_Policy_CONSULTATION_DRAFT.pdf)
- Register data is often wrong: a One Housing review found over 800 entries of incorrect asbestos risk data. — [Roofing Today](https://roofingtoday.co.uk/landlord-review-unearths-hundreds-of-incorrect-asbestos-records)
- Some councils publish block-level asbestos registers as web pages (e.g. Sunderland, Jopling House). — [Sunderland City Council](https://www.sunderland.gov.uk/article/27945/Jopling-House-Asbestos-Register)

### Inferences
- A small contractor does not buy asbestos register software. At best they get a per-property login or a PDF from the landlord. In practice the asbestos report reaches them as an email attachment, which is exactly the content an email RAG tool would index.
- Because the register lives in the landlord's system, a contractor tool should treat the landlord's latest survey as the source of truth and show its date, not answer from memory.

### Gaps
- Shire Systems, Opus/Codeshare, C365Cloud, mobile asbestos register apps and surveyor tools (e.g. Survey Pro): not researched in this pass. No prices found for any asbestos software.
- Unknown how often London boroughs actually give small subcontractors portal logins vs emailing PDFs.

## 3. General AI email and document tools a small firm could use (Gemini in Gmail, Copilot in Outlook, NotebookLM, etc.)

### Takeaway
Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 now bundle AI that can summarise and search email. But "ask a question across my inbox" in Gmail (AI Overviews) is limited to US English on paid consumer plans, attachment answers are not confirmed, and NotebookLM caps notebooks at 50 sources. Microsoft 365 Copilot supports Romanian and costs about £16 per user per month on top of a Microsoft 365 plan (one UK source). None organises email and attachments per property out of the box.

### Cited Findings
- Google Workspace: Gemini in Gmail ("find, summarise and write emails") is listed on all plans including Starter; full Gemini in Docs/Drive etc. starts at Business Standard; "Gemini Notebook" (NotebookLM) has basic access on Starter and expanded access (5x more notebooks, queries and sources) on Standard and above. The pricing page did not show GBP amounts when fetched. — [Google Workspace pricing](https://workspace.google.com/pricing?hl=en-GB_uk)
- Business Standard UK price reported as £13.44 per user per month (from £11.52) after Gemini was bundled in early 2025 (secondary source, Jan 2025; may be out of date). Gemini add-on charges ended 17 March 2025. — [Brighton Chamber notice](https://brightonchamber.co.uk/notice/7269); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/01/15/google-workspace-gemini-price/)
- Gmail "AI Overviews" answer natural-language questions from your email (Google's example: finding a plumber who quoted a bathroom renovation last year). Google's support page lists this for Google AI Pro/Ultra, English, US only. No source confirms answers draw on PDF attachments. The Gemini side panel in Gmail may no longer be shown to some US AI Pro/Ultra users. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gmail/gmail-gemini); [Gmail Help: Gemini features](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/16831098)
- NotebookLM: 100 notebooks, up to 50 sources per notebook, 500,000 words per source, 200 MB per upload; over 50 languages in the browser (Romanian not named in the snippet); Workspace admins must enable it. No official way found to add Gmail messages as sources. Third-party claims of 600 sources per notebook conflict with Google's FAQ. — [NotebookLM FAQ](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16269187); [NotebookLM general info](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/14278184?hl=en); [Fast.io (third party)](https://fast.io/resources/notebooklm-file-limit.md)
- Microsoft 365 Copilot Business: for organisations with up to 300 users on Business Basic/Standard/Premium; US list $21 per user per month (promotional $18 reported, end date disputed). One UK guide gives £16.10 per user per month (promo £13.80 until 31 Mar 2026), and £25.70 total per user with Business Standard (£9.60). From 1 July 2026 "Business Standard with Copilot" and "Business Premium with Copilot" bundles list at $23.50 and $32. GBP figures from a single third-party source. — [Compare the Cloud](https://www.comparethecloud.net/articles/getting-microsoft-365-copilot-working-for-a-20-person-uk-company-without-an-enterprise-agreement); [Office Watch](https://office-watch.com/2025/microsoft-365-copilot-business-ai-add-on/); [Collab365](https://spaces.collab365.com/posts/microsoft-turns-copilot-bundles-for-small-business-fDfsA1)
- Microsoft 365 Copilot lists Romanian among supported prompt/response languages (added in a 2024 batch of 12). — [Microsoft support: supported languages](https://support.microsoft.com/topic/94518d61-644b-4118-9492-617eea4801d8); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-365-copilot-officially-adds-support-for-12-more-languages/)

### Inferences
- A firm already on Microsoft 365 can get Romanian-capable Copilot over Outlook and OneDrive for about £16 per user per month extra. That is the strongest "good enough" rival. A firm on Gmail gets weaker inbox Q&A in the UK today (based on the US-English limit above).
- These tools search by keyword and meaning, not by property. A question like "where is the asbestos in Flat 12, X House" depends on the address being written the same way in every email and on attachments being read. Grouping by property, handling address variants and citing the exact survey page is the gap a new tool could fill (inference, not tested).
- NotebookLM's 50-source cap and no Gmail import make it a manual, per-property workaround at best.

### Gaps
- Superhuman, Shortwave, ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects: not researched in this pass (prices, attachment handling, Romanian).
- Not tested whether Copilot in Outlook answers from PDF attachments (e.g. scanned asbestos surveys).
- Current official GBP prices for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 not confirmed from vendor pages.
- Construction document AI (Trunk Tools, Document Crunch, Archdesk, Kojo, Buildots): not researched; these are believed to target larger main contractors and projects, not per-flat repairs (unverified).

## 4. AI products aimed at social housing repairs, voids and compliance

### Takeaway
There is a growing set of AI tools for social housing, but all found are sold to landlords: void prioritisation (Made Tech), void management (Unify.ai), AI inspections (Property Inspect, Fixflo Aidenn), damp and mould detection (SPOTR with Stonewater), and a regulation knowledge base (HousingAI). None found is aimed at small contractors or at their own emails.

### Cited Findings
- Made Tech Voids: AI-enabled void prioritisation; G-Cloud listing £6,750 to £60,000 per licence per year; savings figures come from a simulation. — [Made Tech Voids](https://www.madetech.com/saas-products/voids/); [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/278682754426814)
- Unify.ai markets void management to social housing, promising to cut void turnaround "from weeks into days" (vendor claim). — [Unify.ai](https://unify.ai/solutions/social-housing/void-management)
- Property Inspect launched "Inspect AI", AI-assisted inspection reporting, marketed to housing providers. — [Property Inspect](https://propertyinspect.com/eu/health-safety/)
- Fixflo (Aareon) offers Aidenn Inspect, AI property inspections. — [Fixflo](https://www.fixflo.com/aidenn-ai-inspections)
- Stonewater spent a year with SPOTR training AI on UK homes to spot damp and mould. — [Property Industry Eye](https://propertyindustryeye.com/affordable-housing-provider-using-ai-to-spot-damp-and-mould/); [Inside Housing](https://management.insidehousing.co.uk/home/how-ai-is-helping-landlords-act-faster-on-damp-and-mould-91003)
- HousingAI launched about 30 April 2026: a paid AI knowledge platform on English social housing law, regulation and best practice, built with Healthy Homes Hub, input from Clarion, Aster, Hyde and Anchor, legal commentary from Anthony Collins, hosted on UK AWS. Aimed at landlords and local authorities. Sources conflict on CEO (Mike Ellis in Dec 2025, Phil Shelton later). — [IT Brief UK](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/housingai-launches-ai-knowledge-platform-for-social-housing); [Housing Technology](https://www.housing-technology.com/housingai-launched-by-healthy-homes-hub/); [Bdaily](https://www.bdaily.co.uk/articles/2025/12/14/housingai-names-chief-executive-ahead-of-2026-launch)

### Inferences
- The AI money in social housing is flowing to landlords. The contractor side, especially small firms, is unserved by purpose-built AI (based on everything found above; absence of evidence, not proof).
- HousingAI shows that a domain-specific RAG product for English social housing can be sold, but it answers "what does the regulation say", not "what does my inbox say about this flat".

### Gaps
- Voyager, Repairs Online and other AI repairs-triage tools: not researched in this pass.
- No independent evaluations of any of these AI tools found.

## 5. Gaps a small contractor cannot fill today, and what would make them switch

### Takeaway
No product found lets a small contractor ask, in Romanian, "where is the asbestos in this flat?" and get an answer from their own emails and attachments, grouped by property, with a link to the source survey. Landlord systems hold the data but are not the contractor's; trade job tools cost £45-£105 per user and don't index email; Gemini/Copilot search email but not by property, and Gmail's inbox Q&A is US-English only.

### Cited Findings
- Asbestos data reaches contractors per job through landlord systems or attachments (TEAMS portal, Access Maintain, Asbestos SMART). — [TEAMS portal](https://www.teams-software.co.uk/Portal); [Access Maintain](https://help-accessmaintain.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/12889794-managing-asbestos-reports); [Asbestos SMART](https://d32j0cd29uv9q5.cloudfront.net/asbestos-smart-take-a-look-at-the-features-and-benefits)
- Gmail AI inbox questions: US English, paid plans only. — [Gmail Help](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/16831098)
- Copilot supports Romanian, about £16 per user per month extra (UK, third-party). — [Microsoft support](https://support.microsoft.com/topic/94518d61-644b-4118-9492-617eea4801d8); [Compare the Cloud](https://www.comparethecloud.net/articles/getting-microsoft-365-copilot-working-for-a-20-person-uk-company-without-an-enterprise-agreement)
- Asbestos records can be wrong, so dates and sources matter. — [Roofing Today](https://roofingtoday.co.uk/landlord-review-unearths-hundreds-of-incorrect-asbestos-records)

### Inferences (all guesses to test with real contractors)
- Reasons a small firm might pick a new tool over Gemini/Copilot: (1) answers organised by property (address matching across "Flat 12", "12 X House", job numbers); (2) reads PDF and scanned survey attachments and cites page and date; (3) Romanian UI and answers, with English source quotes kept as they are; (4) works on WhatsApp/phone on site; (5) one flat price below a job management tool (e.g. under £16 per user, the Copilot benchmark); (6) no IT setup, works with Gmail or Outlook; (7) safety framing: always shows survey date and says "check the landlord's current register" rather than giving a confident answer.
- Main risk: firms already on Microsoft 365 may find Copilot good enough; the product must clearly beat it on per-property search and Romanian.

### Gaps
- No interviews or surveys of Romanian-run contractors on what tools they use today.
- Not checked whether landlords' contracts allow contractors to put landlord documents (asbestos surveys) into third-party AI tools (data protection, confidentiality). This could block adoption and needs research.
