# Existing (non-AI-first) software for UK construction firms and tradespeople (checked 7 Nov 2026)

All vendor pricing pages below were fetched on 7 Nov 2026 unless marked otherwise. Prices are ex VAT unless stated. "Unverified" means the claim comes from a secondary or low-quality source, or could not be confirmed on the vendor's own page.

## Construction management platforms (Procore, Buildertrend/CoConstruct, Fieldwire, Houzz Pro, Contractor Foreman, Causeway, COINS)

### Takeaway
The big platforms split in two: enterprise tools priced by quote (Procore on annual construction volume, Causeway and COINS as UK ERP / commercial suites costing tens of thousands) and per-seat tools for smaller builders (Fieldwire from $39/user/month, Houzz Pro UK from £139/month). Only the UK ERPs (COINS/Access, Causeway, Sage, Eque2) handle CIS natively; the US-born platforms do not mention CIS, VAT or MTD on their pricing pages. AI is being added fast at the top end (Procore Helix, Agent Builder, agents for RFIs/submittals) but is mostly absent or unannounced on small-builder platforms.

### Cited Findings

**Procore (large and mid firms, general contractors)**
- Priced as "an upfront annual fee by product", based on Annual Turnover defined as total value of construction work; unlimited users and unlimited storage; no GBP figures published, quote via sales (+44 0800 368 7703) — [Procore UK pricing](https://www.procore.com/en-gb/pricing)
- Web app languages (UK support FAQ table): English (several regions), French (CA, FR), German, Japanese, Spanish (LatAm, Spain), Simplified Chinese, Polish, Portuguese (BR). Romanian is not listed — [Procore support FAQ](https://en-gb.support.procore.com/faq/what-languages-are-available-in-procore?mt-view=f1)
- Groundbreak, 15 Oct 2025: new AI built into Procore Helix (its "intelligence layer"); Agent Builder in open beta for all customers (agents made from natural-language prompts); pre-built agents incl. an RFI Creation Agent — [BusinessWire press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251015796723/en/Procore-Advances-the-Future-of-Construction-with-New-AI-Innovations-at-Groundbreak-2025)
- 2026: agents for submittals, RFIs, daily logs, built on embedded Datagrid layer, private beta, paid rollout on credit-consumption pricing, human approval required (unverified, aggregator summary; no Procore release found) — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/proptech/news/procore-deploys-ai-workflow-agents-to-automate-rfis-submittals-and-daily-logs-6def3197-9118-40ff-8b4c-dc20e148314f/); claim of 50% less RFI time is a target quoted from a Buro Happold CTO, not a measured result — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/proptech/news/procore-deploys-agentic-ai-in-its-common-data-environment-to-cut-rfi-work-by-50-33e7d3ba-825c-4f8e-9575-73ede1bff844/)
- April 2026 Procore-sourced piece describes Helix as the layer behind AI across the platform — [SMACNA brief](https://www.smacna.org/news/executive-news-brief/article/2026/04/17/meet-procore-helix--ai-that-builds-with-you)

**Buildertrend (incl. CoConstruct) (US residential builders and remodellers, mid design-and-build)**
- No official UK/GBP pricing found; third-party estimates are in USD, roughly $339 to $1,099/month (unverified, competitor/aggregator sources) — [Projul (competitor)](https://www.projul.com/blog/buildertrend-pricing-analysis-2026/); [Capterra pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/70092/Buildertrend/pricing/)

- Site languages selector: English (AU, IE, UK), French, German, Czech, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Polish. No Romanian. AI: only a "Field Intelligence" nav link, no detail — [Fieldwire pricing](https://www.fieldwire.com/pricing/)

**Houzz Pro UK (small builders, renovators, designers; lead-gen + business software)**
- Basic free (directory listing, lead inbox); Pro £139/month after 30-day trial (1 seat, up to £500,000 annual project volume); Custom on quote (up to 10 seats, unlimited volume); extra users £47/user/month (Pro), £59 (Custom); Advertising from £119/month; card payments 2.5%, Bacs 1% — [Houzz Pro UK pricing](https://www.houzz.co.uk/houzz-pro/pricing)
- Pro: estimates, invoicing, 3D floor planner, online payments, CRM; Custom adds takeoffs, schedule, selections, daily logs, budgets, change orders. Client dashboard, QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations. No AI, language, VAT or CIS mention on the page — [Houzz Pro UK pricing](https://www.houzz.co.uk/houzz-pro/pricing)

**Contractor Foreman**
- Pricing page returned no content when fetched; no data gathered — [contractorforeman.com/pricing](https://contractorforeman.com/pricing/)

**Causeway and COINS / Access Construction (UK ERP, large main contractors and housebuilders)**
- Causeway's accounting product is described as for construction businesses, "especially commercial management teams and large main contractors in the UK" — [Capterra: Causeway Project Accounting](https://www.capterra.com/p/227780/Causeway-Project-Accounting/)
- UK tools that handle CIS natively are ERP/commercial systems (Access Coins Evo, Eque2 Construct, Causeway), not field apps (secondary source) — [Compare the Cloud](https://www.comparethecloud.net/articles/construction-project-software-uk-contractors-cis-rams)
- COINS called the most widely used construction-native ERP in the UK (housebuilding, social housing, main contracting); estimated cost roughly £40K to £150K for SMBs vs £15K to £100K for Access/Sage (estimates, not vendor prices) — [ERP Research UK](https://www.erpresearch.com/en-gb/best-erp-software-for-construction-industry)
- Causeway AI: only found a webinar aimed at pre-construction teams; no AI aimed at small builders — [Causeway webinar](https://campaign.causeway.com/ondemand/pre-con/pcla/introduction-to-true-cloud-pre-construction)

### Inferences
- A small Romanian-run builder (1 to 15 people) would not realistically buy Procore, Causeway or COINS: they are priced and built for main contractors. The real competition at that size is Houzz Pro, Fieldwire and the trades apps below.
- US-born platforms (Buildertrend, Fieldwire, Houzz Pro) do not advertise CIS or MTD; UK builders would need Xero/QuickBooks for that. This is a gap an assistant could fill by tying jobs to CIS deductions.

### Gaps
- No official Buildertrend UK price, Contractor Foreman price, or COINS/Causeway list prices (quote-only).
- Could not confirm 2026 Procore agent rollout from Procore's own newsroom.
- Fieldwire product languages: FAQ text not retrieved; only the website language selector was seen.

## UK trades and field service apps (Tradify, ServiceM8, Powered Now, YourTradebase, Fergus, Jobber, Xero/QuickBooks)

### Takeaway
Trades apps are cheap (roughly £25 to £45 per user per month), cover quote, job, schedule, invoice, payments and Xero/QuickBooks sync, and most now ship small AI helpers (text writing, receipt/bill reading, AI receptionists, voice job updates). Only Powered Now advertises HMRC MTD and VAT (with CIS) submission in-app; others rely on Xero/QuickBooks for CIS and MTD.

### Cited Findings

**Tradify (NZ origin; small trades businesses)**
- Per user per month, GBP: Lite £34, Pro £37, Plus £44, Custom on request; 14-day trial, monthly, no contract; SMS 10p; Instant Website £10/month — [Tradify UK pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/pricing)
- Lite: jobs, scheduling (Google Calendar), quotes, invoices, payments, Xero/Sage/QuickBooks Online sync. Pro adds photos/files, online quote acceptance, progress invoicing, timesheets, subcontractor scheduling, compliance certificates — [Tradify UK pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/pricing)
- AI on Plus only ("SmartTools"): SmartRead for Bills (AI cost capture), SmartWrite (job descriptions). No CIS/VAT/MTD or language info on page — [Tradify UK pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/uk/pricing)
- Older directory prices conflict (Lite £34 for 1 to 3 users, Pro £28 for 4+) — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/152413/tradify)

**ServiceM8 (AU origin; small field service firms)**
- Monthly GBP, priced by jobs not users (unlimited users): Free £0 (30 jobs), Starter £25 (50 jobs), Growing £59 (150), Premium £119 (500), Premium Plus £269 (1,500+); extra SMS 5p; extra jobs 15p (Premium Plus) — [ServiceM8 UK pricing](https://www.servicem8.com/uk/pricing)
- AI: AI Writing Helper (10/day on Free, unlimited paid), Office Agent Standard on Starter+, Office Agent Pro £29/month; AI Image Generation, Smart Report Helper, AI Quote & Invoice listed. No language or UK tax info in the part read — [ServiceM8 UK pricing](https://www.servicem8.com/uk/pricing)

**Powered Now (UK-built; small trades)**
- Ex VAT: Business £23.33/month (single user, £280/yr), Professional £26.67/user/month (£320/yr), Premium £33.33/user/month (£400/yr); 14-day trial, no contract — [Powered Now pricing](https://www.powerednow.com/pricing)
- Business includes MTD for Income Tax ("HMRC-recognised for Making Tax Digital"); Professional adds VAT return submission (page says this includes CIS), Xero/QuickBooks, certificates, time tracking, team chat; Premium adds customer portal, auto chasing, drag-and-drop scheduling, drawings — [Powered Now pricing](https://www.powerednow.com/pricing)
- AI (Premium): AI text writing for quotes/messages, AI receipt and cost document scanner. PN Receptionist add-on £29/month (speech) or £39 (speech + text), "natural British voice". Support "UK-based"; no languages listed — [Powered Now pricing](https://www.powerednow.com/pricing)

**YourTradebase (UK-built; small trades)**
- One plan £29/month (+VAT), two months free yearly, extra users £15/month, 30-day trial then free limited tier — [YourTradebase pricing](http://www.yourtradebase.com/pricing/)
- Quotes, scheduling, job sheets, invoices, card payments, Xero/QuickBooks, iOS/Android. No AI, CIS, MTD or language mention — [YourTradebase pricing](http://www.yourtradebase.com/pricing/)

**Fergus (NZ origin; plumbing/electrical/trades firms)**
- GBP per month: Essentials from £39, Professional from £45, Enterprise (10+ staff) custom; timesheet users from £12; contractor users £2/user/day; card 1.6% + 20p — [Fergus UK pricing](https://fergus.com/uk/pricing/)
- AI products: Fergus Assistant (ask about jobs, costs), Fergus Go Assistant (type or speak on site to update jobs), Fergus AI (admin), AI email ingestion of supplier invoices. No CIS/VAT/MTD; site in English variants only — [Fergus UK pricing](https://fergus.com/uk/pricing/)

**Jobber (Canada; home service businesses)**
- No GBP prices on the en-GB page; "All prices in USD": Core $29 to $49/month (1 user); Connect from $99 to $139 (1 user); Grow from $149 to $199; Plus from $399 (5 users); extra users $29 — [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing/)
- AI: Jobber AI Voice (quotes, schedules, messages by voice), AI Chat over business data, AI Receptionist 24/7 calls/texts ($29/month add-on, included in Plus), AI custom reports, AI Voice for field workers that logs notes "in any language" with instant translation — [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing/)
- App in Spanish for some team members, not admins/owners. No UK tax mention — [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing/)

**Xero / QuickBooks (accounting layer most trades apps sync to)**
- Xero CIS: mark contacts as subcontractors, send deduction statements; verifying subcontractors needs the paid CIS Contractor add-on — [Xero CIS guide](https://www.xero.com/uk/guides/construction-industry-scheme/)
- Xero domestic reverse charge (DRC) VAT feature — [Xero DRC](https://www.xero.com/uk/features-and-tools/accounting-software/domestic-reverse-charge/)
- MTD for Income Tax applies to CIS sole-trader subcontractors from 6 April 2026 if qualifying income over £50,000; monthly CIS returns are a separate duty (secondary source) — [Clear Books builders MTD](https://www.clearbooks.co.uk/making-tax-digital-income-tax/builders/)
- QuickBooks has a CIS centre; some accountants find it less smooth than Xero for CIS-heavy firms (secondary source) — [Pulse Accountants](https://pulse-accountants.co.uk/blog/best-accounting-software-for-construction-company-2026)

### Inferences
- The AI added so far is narrow: writing help, receipt reading, phone answering, voice job updates. None advertise a full assistant that runs quotes, CIS and client chat in another language.
- Price anchor for a new product: £25 to £45 per user per month, with AI receptionists as £29 to £39 add-ons.

### Gaps
- Official GBP prices for Jobber not published on UK page.
- Did not verify whether Tradify, Fergus, YourTradebase handle CIS on invoices beyond passing data to Xero.

## Which tools support Romanian (app or support)?

### Takeaway
None of the mainstream tools checked lists Romanian in the app or support. Procore and Fieldwire support Polish but not Romanian; the UK trades apps list no languages beyond English (Jobber offers partial Spanish). Romanian appears only in small niche tools.

### Cited Findings
- Procore web app: no Romanian; Polish supported — [Procore support FAQ](https://en-gb.support.procore.com/faq/what-languages-are-available-in-procore?mt-view=f1)
- Fieldwire site languages include Polish and Czech, not Romanian — [Fieldwire pricing](https://www.fieldwire.com/pricing/)
- Jobber: Spanish app for team members only; AI Voice for field workers logs notes "in any language" with translation — [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/en-gb/pricing/)
- Powered Now, Tradify, YourTradebase, Fergus, Houzz Pro, ServiceM8 pricing pages list no languages (Fergus: English regions only; Powered Now stresses "UK-based" support and a "British voice") — [Powered Now](https://www.powerednow.com/pricing); [Fergus](https://fergus.com/uk/pricing/)
- Niche tools claiming Romanian (vendor claims, unverified): Buildable (English, Polish, Romanian, Portuguese, Spanish) — [buildable.pages.dev](https://buildable.pages.dev); CraftedEX (12 interface languages incl. Romanian) — [GetApp UK](https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2142871/craftedex)
- Abilo, a construction ERP built by Romanian firm Obi Systems, pitched at the UK market; Romanian UI not confirmed — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/romanian-entrepreneur-uk-construction-erp-2025)

### Inferences
- Romanian language support is a clear gap among established tools; the closest are Polish UIs and generic translation features.

### Gaps
- Did not check language settings inside each app or support channels directly; "no Romanian" for trades apps is based on absence from pricing pages, not a vendor statement.

## What do users complain about (Capterra, G2, Trustpilot)?

### Takeaway
UK trades apps score high on Trustpilot (4.7 to 4.8), with few complaints, mostly missing reports, Xero sync glitches and slow support. Larger platforms like Buildertrend draw complaints about price, renewal rises and a steep learning curve for small firms.

### Cited Findings
- Tradify Trustpilot: 4.8/5, 625 reviews (209 in last 12 months), 3% one-star; complaints: two days without support (6 Nov 2026), Xero sync "can be a bit fiddly, and sometimes it doubles things up" (29 Jan 2026), no itemised P&L report and poor value at £30/month (28 Oct 2026) — [Trustpilot Tradify](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradifyhq.com)
- Powered Now Trustpilot: 4.7, 571 reviews, about 7% one/two-star; negative review text not visible; company "hasn't replied to negative reviews" — [Trustpilot Powered Now](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/powerednow.com)
- Buildertrend: price is the most common complaint; small builders say cost is hard to justify; a Capterra reviewer reports a 75% rise without notice (competitor summary) — [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/best-buildertrend-alternatives/); G2 users cite complexity and steep learning curve — [G2 Buildertrend](https://www.g2.com/products/buildertrend/reviews); setup time-consuming, needs ongoing training — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/buildertrend/); smaller contractors may find price and implementation hard to justify — [Connecteam review](https://connecteam.com/reviews/buildertrend/)
- Buildertrend G2 rating 4.2 to 4.3/5 (varies by snapshot); BBB 1.08/5 from 12 reviews — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/buildertrend); [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ne/omaha/profile/computer-software/buildertrend-solutions-0714-300027310/customer-reviews)

### Inferences
- For trades apps the pain is less cost and more gaps (reporting, CIS, integrations). For platforms it is cost and complexity. A simple, Romanian-speaking assistant could pitch on low onboarding effort.

### Gaps
- Capterra UK review pages returned 403; could not read Capterra cons for Tradify, Fergus, ServiceM8, YourTradebase.
- No UK trade press (Construction News, ProBuilder) coverage checked in this pass.
- Third-party UK price trackers (whito.co.uk, kaeltripton.com) appeared in searches but look low quality; not used.
