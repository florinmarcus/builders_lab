# Small and medium UK commercial contractors: procedures, software and admin pains

All URLs checked on 19 Nov 2026, unless a date is given. "Evidence" means a source says it. "Inference" means our reading of the evidence. Many software sources are vendor marketing. They are marked as such.

## Procedures: how a small commercial contractor runs a job (tender to defects period)

### Takeaway
The job runs on a fixed legal and contract frame. CDM 2015 sets the safety paperwork (construction phase plan, inductions, F10, checks on subcontractors). JCT sets the money and time (monthly valuations, payment notices, pay less notices, practical completion, rectification period). Site managers spend much of their time on RAMS, inductions, daily records and progress updates. In occupied buildings, they also coordinate with the client's own staff and residents.

### Cited Findings

**Example contractor (FDT Construction Services Ltd)**
- FDT's homepage says it does commercial new build and refurbishment, and "occupied and fully operational job sites are never a problem". It says it can plan, manage and build multi-phase jobs, and offers an "end-to-end client experience" covering communication, budgeting, staffing and on-site organisation. The homepage names no accreditations and no software. — [FDT Construction Services](https://www.fdtconstructionservices.co.uk/)

**CDM 2015: principal contractor duties (official HSE guidance)**
- The principal contractor must prepare a written construction phase plan "before the construction phase begins", then implement, review and revise it. — [HSE: Principal contractors](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/principal-contractors.htm)
- It must make sure "all workers have site-specific inductions", provide "suitable welfare facilities ... from the start", and prevent unauthorised site access. — [HSE: Principal contractors](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/principal-contractors.htm)
- It must "check that anyone they appoint has the skills, knowledge, experience" (this is the legal basis for subcontractor vetting). It must also consult workers and liaise with the client and the principal designer. — [HSE: Principal contractors](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/principal-contractors.htm)
- A project is notifiable (F10) if work lasts more than 30 working days with more than 20 workers at the same time at any point, or exceeds 500 person days. Weekends and holidays with work count. The client holds the duty to notify, but may ask someone else (often the contractor) to do it. Notice goes online or on paper before the construction phase begins. — [HSE CDM FAQ: Notifications](https://hse.gov.uk/Construction/cdm/faq/notifications.htm); [Designing Buildings: F10](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Notifiable_project_F10_form)
- The F10 includes site address, principal contractor details, planned start and duration, maximum number of workers and number of contractors. — [Designing Buildings: F10](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Notifiable_project_F10_form)
- Under CDM 2015, the health and safety file for notifiable projects is compiled by the principal designer (with information from the contractor). — [Fenwick Elliott: contractual liabilities post completion](https://www.fenwickelliott.com/sites/default/files/fenwick_elliott_-_contractual_liabilities_post_completion.pdf)

**Construction Act and JCT payment cycle (example: JCT SBC/Q 2016, from JCT's own guidance, 16 Oct 2023)**
- Interim valuation dates are set in the contract particulars, normally monthly. By each date the contractor may send an application for payment showing the sum it thinks is due and how it was calculated. — [JCT Explains: Interim Payments](https://corporate.jctltd.co.uk/jct-explains-interim-payments/)
- Due date: 7 days after the valuation date. The contract administrator must issue the interim certificate (payment notice) within 5 days of the due date, the maximum the Construction Act allows (s.110A). — [JCT Explains: Interim Payments](https://corporate.jctltd.co.uk/jct-explains-interim-payments/)
- If no certificate is issued, the contractor's application becomes the payment notice (s.110B). — [JCT Explains: Interim Payments](https://corporate.jctltd.co.uk/jct-explains-interim-payments/)
- A pay less notice must be given no later than 5 days before the final date for payment. Without one, the full notified sum must be paid. Final date for payment: 14 days after the due date. Late payment carries interest at 5% above Bank of England base rate. — [JCT Explains: Interim Payments](https://corporate.jctltd.co.uk/jct-explains-interim-payments/)
- The contractor may suspend work if payment is still outstanding 7 days after a notice of intention to suspend; adjudication is the alternative. — [JCT Explains: Interim Payments](https://corporate.jctltd.co.uk/jct-explains-interim-payments/)
- The JCT 2024 editions of Design and Build and Intermediate added a new clause 8.13 with Construction Act timings for payment notices, pay less notices and final dates. — [Burges Salmon (via search snippet)](https://www.burges-salmon.com/?p=4250)
- Missing a payment or pay less notice leads to "smash and grab" adjudications, still an active dispute area in 2026. — [Fenwick Elliott Insight issue 101 (2026)](https://www.fenwickelliott.com/knowledge-hub/insight/issue-101/)
- NBS published contract administrator guidance (Nov 2024) that includes JCT MW/MWD 2024 and lists pay less notices among its forms. I did not get the MW 2024 timings from it. — [NBS contract administrator guidance, Nov 2024](https://www.thenbs.com/hubfs/Website%20Assets/Files/Newsroom%20files/Specifiers/contract-administrator-guidance-november-2024.pdf)

**Practical completion, handover and defects**
- JCT forms (apart from the Major Project form) do not define practical completion. At practical completion the final account process usually starts, half the retention is released, and the client takes possession (and insurance). — [Local Government Lawyer: JCT 101](https://localgovernmentlawyer.co.uk/sharpeedge/810-sharpeedge-projects/48733-jct-101-time-and-punishment); [Designing Buildings: Practical completion](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Practical_completion_certificate)
- The defects liability period is called the "rectification period" in JCT (including 2024). It usually starts at practical completion and runs 6 to 12 months. — [Designing Buildings: Defects liability period](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Defect_liability_period)
- A typical retention split is 2.5% released at practical completion and 2.5% at the end of the defects period. Sources disagree on splits, so check the contract. — [Designing Buildings: Practical completion](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Practical_completion_certificate)
- O&M manuals, as-built drawings, collateral warranties and the health and safety file are often express preconditions to practical completion. — [Fenwick Elliott: contractual liabilities post completion](https://www.fenwickelliott.com/sites/default/files/fenwick_elliott_-_contractual_liabilities_post_completion.pdf); [RICS Construction Journal: Practical completion](https://ww3.rics.org/uk/en/journals/construction-journal/practical-completion.html)
- Under unamended JCT DB 2016 the employer issues the Practical Completion Statement. — [Fenwick Elliott](https://www.fenwickelliott.com/sites/default/files/fenwick_elliott_-_contractual_liabilities_post_completion.pdf)

**Day-to-day site manager duties (job adverts, Nov 2026)**
- A Sheffield refurbishment site manager role (posted 10 Nov 2026, temporary, £250 to £260/day) lists: "Review and challenge subcontractor RAMS", "Complete site inductions and maintain accurate health and safety records", "Monitor workmanship and complete quality assurance checks", "Hold regular site meetings and provide progress updates", and "Work alongside customer liaison teams to minimise disruption to residents". It requires SMSTS, CSCS, First Aid, Asbestos Awareness, and values JCT or NEC experience. No software is named. — [The Construction Index job](https://www.theconstructionindex.co.uk/construction-jobs/view/-/site-manager-1665/200409)
- Other Nov 2026 adverts (search snippets): a Leeds office refurbishment (CAT A and CAT B fit-out) in a building that stays occupied during works; roles that keep RAMS, daily reports and quality documentation; a Kent role that maintains site diaries and works in occupied buildings. — [Construction Job Board: site manager jobs](https://www.constructionjobboard.co.uk/categories/1241/site-manager-jobs/); [CIOB Jobs](https://ciobjobs.com/job/164946/site-manager-7265/)
- A London office fit-out site manager advert (via a government job board) lists site inductions, toolbox talks, RAMS reviews, audits, CDM compliance, and experience in "live or constrained commercial environments". — [Find a Job (DWP)](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/17715460)
- Fit-out and refurbishment site manager and PM adverts (CIOB Jobs, autumn 2026) ask for live refurbishment experience: live hotels, medical centres, occupied offices. Salaries quoted: £50K to £75K. — [CIOB Jobs: fit-out site manager](https://ciobjobs.com/job/161835/fit-out-site-manager-25/); [CIOB Jobs: PM commercial fit-out](https://ciobjobs.com/job/159894/project-manager-commercial-fit-out-refurbishment/); [CIOB Jobs: medical centre](https://ciobjobs.com/job/164614/site-manager-high-end-medical-centre/)
- QS adverts for smaller contractors stress estimating, take-offs, pricing, cost management, contracts, purchase orders, suppliers and subcontractors, and "strong Excel skills". — [Totaljobs: QS jobs](https://www.totaljobs.com/jobs/quantity-surveyor); [CIOB Jobs: QS](https://ciobjobs.com/job/158863/quantity-surveyor-6865/)

### Inferences
- The procedures form a clear timeline we can use for demo scenarios: tender (BoQ pricing, subcontractor quotes) -> contract (JCT) -> pre-start (construction phase plan, F10, RAMS vetting) -> site (inductions, diaries, RFIs, variations, progress meetings) -> money (monthly application, payment notice, pay less notice) -> practical completion (snags, O&M, H&S file) -> rectification period (defects, final retention).
- Payment deadlines are short and strict (5 days, 14 days). A missed notice can cost the full sum. This makes "remind me of payment deadlines and draft the notice" a strong, concrete demo.
- In occupied buildings the site manager also deals with the client's FM team or resident liaison. The adverts say this. Permit-to-work details came from no source I could verify (gap).
- FDT's own site is thin. For a demo we should describe FDT as an example type, not claim knowledge of its tools.

### Gaps
- JCT Minor Works 2024 exact payment timings: not confirmed. The JCT SBC/Q timings above are an example; check the MW 2024 contract or JCT guidance.
- No source found on the tender process for small contractors (BoQ pricing, subcontractor enquiries, tender returns). Theory is well known, but no URL collected.
- No source collected on RFI and variation (change control) procedures in JCT, or on permit-to-work systems in occupied buildings (hot works, isolations, client FM sign-off).
- The HSE page on the health and safety file, and CDM changes since 2015, were not checked (one HSE URL returned 404).

## Software small and medium UK commercial contractors use

### Takeaway
Evidence for small firms points to a light stack: accounts in Sage or Xero (often with a construction add-on for CIS and valuations), spreadsheets for valuations and payments, email and WhatsApp for site talk, and sometimes a field app (Fieldwire, PlanRadar, Site Samurai) for snags and drawings. Procore, Access Coins (COINS) and Causeway appear mostly at mid-size and above (case studies start around £18M turnover). Job adverts for small-firm site roles rarely name software at all.

### Cited Findings

**Accounts and finance**
- A UK SME vendor guide (Site Samurai, reviewed 19 Aug 2026; vendor marketing) calls Xero and Sage the "UK SME defaults". It says Access Coins (formerly COINS) is quote-only and suits mid-to-large contractors with a dedicated finance function. It targets SMEs of 10 to 250 staff. — [Site Samurai: Procore alternatives for UK SMEs](https://www.sitesamurai.co.uk/alternatives/procore-alternatives-uk-sme-contractors)
- Sicon Construction is an add-on installed inside Sage 200, covering CIS returns, retentions, applications and valuations (vendor claim). — [Sicon Construction](https://www.sicon.co.uk/product/construction/)
- A Sage reseller says Sage 200 automates CIS: subcontractor verification, deductions and monthly HMRC returns; applications and valuations need the Projects module. — [PKF Smith Cooper: Sage 200 for construction](https://www.pkfscs.co.uk/sectors/sage-200-for-construction/)
- Access Coins case study: Newett Homes, a mid-sized housebuilder, moved from Sage and spreadsheets to Access Coins (vendor content). — [Access Group: Newett Homes](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/construction/case-studies-testimonials/newett-homes/)
- An independent-style review questions whether Access Coins suits SMEs. — [Jibble: Access Coins review](https://www.jibble.io/construction-software-reviews/access-coins-review)
- Causeway appears in a buyer's guide as an option for mid-sized contractors; no SME case study found. — [ERP Research: construction accounting software UK 2026](https://erpresearch.com/en-gb/accounting-software-for-construction)

**Project and field management**
- Procore charges an annual fee based on construction turnover and is not published; the Site Samurai guide calls it Tier 1 software (vendor competitor view). — [Site Samurai](https://www.sitesamurai.co.uk/alternatives/procore-alternatives-uk-sme-contractors)
- A third-party guide claims Procore does not suit businesses under £5M annual revenue (vendor-adjacent, weak). — [Procore alternatives commentary (search result)](https://www.kaeltripton.com/site-management-software-uk/)
- Fieldwire pricing: Basic free; Pro $39, Business $64, Business Plus $89 per user per month (annual billing, USD), per Site Samurai. Another guide quotes £29 to £33 per user per month. — [Site Samurai](https://www.sitesamurai.co.uk/alternatives/procore-alternatives-uk-sme-contractors); [BuildersAI comparison](https://buildersai.co.uk/blog/construction-project-management-software-uk)
- Site Samurai: £199/month Professional, £699/month Enterprise, unlimited users, aimed at UK SMEs on JCT or NEC (own pricing). PlanRadar targets defects, snagging, inspections and handover evidence. Payapps targets progress claims across a supply chain. — [Site Samurai](https://www.sitesamurai.co.uk/alternatives/procore-alternatives-uk-sme-contractors)
- Procore UK case studies (vendor content): Kenham, Kensington, about £18M turnover, says it cut up to 10 hours a week of admin; ThirdWay Contracts (about £72M) cut snagging on a typical floor "from half a day to half an hour"; BW: Workplace Experts (about £180M) reports 70% less rework and 40% less admin. — [Procore: Kenham PDF](https://mkt-cdn.procore.com/downloads/casestudies/CaseStudy_Kenham.pdf); [Procore: ThirdWay](https://www.procore.com/en-gb/casestudies/thirdway-contracts); [Procore: BW](https://procore.com/en-gb/casestudies/bw-workplace-experts) (figures from search summaries; the Kenham PDF did not parse when fetched)
- Archdesk (vendor) argues general contractor tools like Procore lack cost, procurement and labour detail for specialist fit-out firms. — [Archdesk: 2026 guide to UK fit-out software](https://archdesk.com/blog/2026-guide-uk-construction-fit-out-software)

**Messaging and communication**
- Construction Management (CIOB's magazine) says consumer messaging apps like WhatsApp are still "widely used as construction site communications and management tools across the majority of UK projects", and urges scrutiny over record-keeping. — [Construction Management: consumer messaging apps](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/consumer-messaging-apps-among-project-teams-thumbs-up-or-thumbs-down/)
- Lawyers writing in Construction Management say WhatsApp messages can be useful evidence in disputes (when a photo was sent and seen), with legal risks too. — [Construction Management: should we use WhatsApp](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/should-we-use-whatsapp-for-site-communications/)

**Industry surveys**
- NBS Digital Construction Report 2025 (Oct 2025, 559 respondents): just over two in five use AI tools, more than four times five years earlier; over half worry about being left behind. Note: about 66.5% of respondents are on the design and specification side, so it says little about small contractors. — [NBS Digital Construction Report 2025](https://www.thenbs.com/digital-construction-report-2025); [NBS: two-fifths now using AI](https://www.thenbs.com/news-and-press/two-fifths-of-industry-now-using-ai-insights-from-the-construction-leaders-summit); [Beale & Co analysis](https://beale-law.com/?p=25704)
- Xpedeon Construction Systems Census 2026 (500 senior staff at UK firms over £50M turnover; vendor survey): duplicate data entry in subcontractor valuations (39%), timesheets and labour (37%), materials received (35%). — [Xpedeon census](https://xpedeon.com/uk-construction-systems-census/)

**Job adverts**
- None of the Nov 2026 site manager, PM and fit-out adverts I opened named Procore, Fieldwire, Asta Powerproject, Bluebeam or MS Project. They name qualifications (SMSTS, CSCS, First Aid), RAMS, CDM and JCT/NEC. — [The Construction Index job](https://www.theconstructionindex.co.uk/construction-jobs/view/-/site-manager-1665/200409); [CIOB Jobs search results](https://ciobjobs.com/job/163860/site-manager-fit-out-north-west-2-hires/)

### Inferences
- For a small firm like FDT, the most likely real stack is: Microsoft 365 or Google (email, Excel, OneDrive/SharePoint), Sage or Xero for accounts, Excel for valuations and trackers, WhatsApp groups per site, PDFs for drawings, and maybe one field app. This is inference from the SME vendor guide and the absence of software in adverts, not direct evidence about FDT.
- Good MCP integration points for a demo, in order of how likely a small firm has them: email and calendar (Outlook/Gmail), files (SharePoint/OneDrive/Google Drive with Excel trackers), accounts (Xero has a public API; Sage too), WhatsApp (hard: WhatsApp Business API only, consumer groups not accessible), and a field app (Procore and Fieldwire have APIs).
- Because messages in WhatsApp groups are often the real record, a demo where the assistant turns a pasted chat export or photos into a site diary entry or a variation record is realistic. Direct live access to consumer WhatsApp groups is not.
- Procore is a fair "if they have it" connector, but mock data should not assume it for a small firm.

### Gaps
- No evidence collected for Asta Powerproject vs MS Project use at small contractors, or for Bluebeam, Aconex, Viewpoint, or safety apps (SafetyCulture/iAuditor, Novade, BuildSafe) at this size.
- No CSCS check tool evidence collected (CSCS has a smart check app; not verified here).
- No CIOB survey on software use at small contractors found.
- Microsoft 365 / SharePoint use by small contractors: assumed, not sourced.

## Similar small UK commercial contractor websites: processes, accreditations and tools

### Takeaway
Small commercial refurbishment contractors sell on three things: low disruption in live buildings (schools, clinics, offices), single-point design and build, and accreditations (CHAS, Constructionline Silver or Gold, ISO 9001/14001/45001, SSIP). None of the sites checked name the software they use.

### Cited Findings
- FDT Construction Services: commercial new build and refurbishment, occupied sites, multi-phase. No accreditations or tools on the homepage. — [FDT](https://www.fdtconstructionservices.co.uk/)
- SMG Building Solutions (Kent and London): office fit-outs, commercial refurbishment, design and build "under one contract", maintenance. Process: Plan, Build, Maintain, Enhance. Accreditations: CHAS, Constructionline Gold, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 (ISO 45001 also shown), Cyber Essentials. Clients named include DPD, Network Rail, MoD. Work done "with minimal operational disruption". No software named. — [SMG Building Solutions](https://smgbuildingsolutions.co.uk/)
- 360CE (Reading): fit-out, phased refurbishment, new build for schools, clinics and offices. Process: consultation, design and specification, construction, compliance and handover. Stresses "minimal disruption" in live school, clinical and office settings and infection control. Lists CHAS, Constructionline, CSCS, IOSH, PASMA, IPAF. No software named. — [360CE](https://360ce.co.uk/)
- C&S Ltd: design and build fit-outs and refurbishments since 1998; CHAS and Constructionline Gold logos. — [C&S Ltd](https://candsltd.co.uk/)
- KRP Developments (Hampshire): CHAS Elite; says it works "under CDM 2015 on all school projects", including an occupied secondary school of about 1,000 pupils. — [KRP Developments](https://krpdevelopments.co.uk/school-refurbishment/)
- Klick Technology: CHAS, Constructionline Gold, SafeContractor; refurbishes schools while occupied. — [Klick Technology](https://klicktechnology.co.uk/school_refurbishment/)
- Bell Group: education maintenance and refurbishment; ISO, CHAS, Constructionline Gold. — [Bell Group](https://www.bellgroup.co.uk/sectors/education/)
- Chas Berger & Son: ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 covering refurbishment, maintenance and new build management. — [Chas Berger](https://chasberger.co.uk/company/awards-accreditations/)
- Accreditation context: Constructionline Gold adds ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 (or equivalent), equality, anti-bribery and full SSIP (CHAS, SMAS, SafeContractor) on top of Silver. Principal contractors and public frameworks often require Silver or Gold. — [Trade2Base guide](https://www.trade2base.com/blog/chas-constructionline-guide); [TenderReady](https://tenderready.co.uk/blog/construction-supplier-prequalification-uk)
- SMAS appeared on a scaffolding firm's site, not on the refurbishment contractors checked. — [Cheshire Scaffolds](https://cheshirescaffoldsltd.co.uk/commercial-scaffolding-manchester-north-west/)

### Inferences
- Accreditations mean recurring paperwork: annual CHAS/Constructionline renewals, ISO audits, policies, insurance certificates, training records. Both the contractor and its subcontractors must keep these current. An assistant that tracks expiry dates and collects evidence is a plausible demo.
- "Minimal disruption in live buildings" is the main selling point, so a demo around client FM communication (out-of-hours works notices, permits, phasing updates) fits the market.
- Contractor websites are self-published. Accreditation claims should be checked on the CHAS/Constructionline registers before any reuse.

### Gaps
- Only seven contractor sites were checked, mostly from one search; none were in the North or Scotland.
- No site described its document control, RFI or payment process in detail.

## Biggest admin pains

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced pain is money admin: subcontractor applications and payments run on spreadsheets and email, take many hours, and cause disputes. Next is scattered communication (WhatsApp and email as the record). Most numbers come from vendors (Payapps, Xpedeon, Procore), so treat them as direction, not fact.

### Cited Findings
- Payapps survey of 127 UK subcontractors (vendor): 55% spend over 20 hours a month on admin; each payment claim takes about 1.5 hours; 67% prepare most claims in spreadsheets; 37% work across several systems depending on the contractor; 73% had routine disputes over payment applications. — [Payapps subcontractor payment research 2026](https://www.payapps.com/blogs-ie/download-subcontractor-payment-research-2026-what-contractors-need-to-know-ie/); [Construction Management: subcontractors blacklisting](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/subcontractors-blacklisting-contractors-who-dont-pay-survey-finds/)
- Survey of 162 UK and Irish construction finance professionals (Payapps): 50% use spreadsheets for subcontractor payments, 46% depend on email, 27% still use pen and paper; only a fifth see applications fully before approval, 22% have little or no visibility. A typical five-person finance team handles 210 subcontractors and spends 73 hours a month on subcontractor payments. — [Construction Management: 210 subcontractors with pen and paper](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/managing-210-subcontractors-with-pen-and-paper-and-a-spreadsheet/); [Construction Intelligence: 73 hours](https://www.constructionintelligence.co.uk/finance/73-hours-a-week-the-admin-burden-behind-constructions-payment-problem/); [SpecFinish](https://specfinish.co.uk/hard-numbers-uk-construction-finance-teams-are-drowning-in-admin/)
- Duplicate data entry in subcontractor valuations (39%), timesheets (37%) and materials (35%) at firms over £50M (Xpedeon, vendor). — [Xpedeon census](https://xpedeon.com/uk-construction-systems-census/)
- WhatsApp is used on most UK projects for site communication and management, raising record-keeping concerns. — [Construction Management](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/consumer-messaging-apps-among-project-teams-thumbs-up-or-thumbs-down/)
- Procore case studies report admin savings (Kenham up to 10 hours a week; BW 40% less admin) and faster snagging (ThirdWay). Vendor content. — [Procore: ThirdWay](https://www.procore.com/en-gb/casestudies/thirdway-contracts); [Procore: BW](https://procore.com/en-gb/casestudies/bw-workplace-experts)
- Missed payment and pay less notices drive "smash and grab" adjudications. — [Fenwick Elliott Insight 101](https://www.fenwickelliott.com/knowledge-hub/insight/issue-101/)
- Site managers must review and challenge subcontractor RAMS, run inductions and keep health and safety records (advert duties). — [The Construction Index job](https://www.theconstructionindex.co.uk/construction-jobs/view/-/site-manager-1665/200409)

### Inferences
- Five demo scenarios with good evidence behind them:
  1. **Payment cycle assistant:** read the contract dates, track each valuation, remind before payment notice and pay less notice deadlines, draft the application from an Excel valuation.
  2. **Subcontractor pack check:** read RAMS, insurance and CSCS/training records from email attachments or a shared folder, flag gaps and expiry dates before start on site.
  3. **Site diary from chat:** turn a day's WhatsApp export, photos and notes into a dated site diary and progress note for the client.
  4. **Variation log:** spot instructions in emails or chats, log them as possible variations with cost and time, and draft a notice to the client.
  5. **Handover pack:** track snags, O&M documents and H&S file information against the practical completion checklist.
- For a small firm the "systems" are mostly email, a shared drive with Excel, accounts software and WhatsApp. Interconnecting these is the realistic value, which fits the "connect, don't replace" goal.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) UK survey of admin hours at small contractors found.
- No survey on WhatsApp use specifically (only expert commentary).
- No evidence on client reporting burden (monthly reports to clients) at small firms.
- RFIs and document control pains are inferred, not sourced, for small firms.
