# Builder 1: small refurbishment firm, council work

Call by Florin, 7 Nov 2026. Notes written the same day from Florin's summary. Anonymised: no names, no council name.

## Business

- Small building business, refurbishment.
- Main client: the local council. Refurbishes council flats.
- The council gives a budget (about 10,000, currency assumed GBP) to cover a batch of properties.
- Uses QuickBooks for accounts. Says accounts are not the problem.

## Pain point

- The council sends many emails with lots of documentation per property.
- He wants it searchable and easy to access.
- Example: asbestos. Does the property have asbestos, and where exactly? Hard to find today. (Florin said "arson"; we think he meant asbestos, to confirm.)

## What this suggests (our inference, not his words)

- First use case: search over his own emails and attachments, organised by property.
- Needs: link every document to a property; read scanned PDFs and tables (asbestos surveys); always cite document and page; never say "no asbestos" when nothing was found.
- Check his council contract before any real documents are used.

## Follow-up questions

- What arrives by email: work orders, asbestos surveys, specifications, photos? PDFs? Scanned or typed?
- How many emails and documents per property and per month?
- What else does he search for: access details, tenant contact, scope of work, deadlines?
- How does he track spending against the council budget per property?
- Does the council also use a portal?
- Does his contract limit sharing council documents or using outside software?
- Could he share a few example documents with personal details removed, to build realistic mock data?
