# Main pitch concepts (short)

Full version: `pitch.md`.

1. **Talk, don't type.** Ask your phone, in Romanian or English, and listen to the answer. You don't read your emails: they are filed by job and the key facts pulled out.
2. **It knows your jobs.** What came in, what the client approved (in their own words), what you're owed and when, what's missing before handover.
3. **It keeps you legal.** Your emails and actions are checked against the rules: payment and pay less notice dates, what to do and who to tell when you're late, asbestos. It warns you before you send.
4. **Every answer shows its source.** The email, who sent it, the date; or the regulation, section and edition. Plain code checks every answer. If we don't know, we say so.
5. **Why not ChatGPT or Google?** They don't know your jobs, and they answer from old training or random web pages. We answer only from your own emails and official English regulations.
6. **Real Romanian, not just translation.** Building terms are checked with a glossary, official UK terms stay in English, and anything sent to clients goes out in proper English.
7. **Safe and private.** Business plan only (Claude Team), with a data agreement. Data in London, nobody trains on it, deleted when you leave. It drafts; you send.
8. **Honest.** Nobody on the market says how often their AI is wrong. We publish our test results. It's checked and cited, not "guaranteed". A person still signs.
