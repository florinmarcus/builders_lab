# Pitch talk: main ideas (draft 1, 21 Nov 2026)

For Florin's talk to builders. Spoken style. To refine. Facts from `research/` and `evals/`; prices from `requirements/business-plan.md`.

## 1. Opening

You didn't become a builder to do paperwork at 10 at night.
Emails, notices, deadlines, who approved what. Miss one and it costs you money.
We built a team of AI agents that does that part with you. You talk to it on your phone, in Romanian or English.

## 2. Why pay us, when you can use ChatGPT or Claude yourself?

You can. For simple things, you should. Here is what you don't get on your own:

- **It knows your jobs.** ChatGPT doesn't know your emails, your client or your dates. Ours files every email by job and remembers what was agreed.
- **It knows the rules.** ChatGPT answers from old training or random web pages. Ours answers from a library of official English regulations, with the section and the date. If it's not in there, it says "I don't know" instead of guessing.
- **It checks itself.** Before you hear an answer, plain computer code checks it. Every fact needs a source. Payment dates are calculated, not guessed. It is not allowed to say "no asbestos". A change counts as approved only if the client's own words say so.
- **It's legal and private.** Free AI can learn from your chats. We only work on business plans, with a data agreement. Your data stays in London and is deleted when you leave.
- **Someone sets it up and stands behind it.** You don't spend weekends wiring connectors. We install it, train you, and answer when it breaks.

In one line: **ChatGPT is a clever stranger. We're a team that knows your jobs, knows the rules, and checks its work.**

## 3. Honest

Nobody on the market tells you how often their AI is wrong. We do.
It's not perfect. That's why it shows you the source, checks itself, and never sends anything without you.
You still sign. We make sure you sign the right thing.

## 4. For one-man bands and small crews

You are the builder, the office and the accountant.

- **Talk, don't type.** Ask on site: "Did the client approve the extra socket?" It answers with the client's own words and the date.
- **No more lost papers.** Every email and attachment is filed by job.
- **Get paid on time.** It tells you when payment notices are due, and what happens if the client misses one.
- **When you're late.** It tells you the next legal step: who to tell, by when, and what you risk. It drafts the email in proper English. You check and send.
- **In Romanian, properly.** UK terms stay in English so you use the right word with clients and councils.

Price: £250 setup, then £49 a month per firm, plus your own Claude Team plan (about £20 per person).

## 5. For small firms with an office (5 to 20 people)

Same tools, but the value is control:

- **One place for every job.** Who said what, what's missing before handover, which subcontractor papers are out of date.
- **Fewer disputes.** Approved changes, with the client's words, ready for the final account.
- **Less risk.** Every email checked against payment and delay rules before it goes out.
- **The boss gets a summary** of how the firm stands today (coming soon).

## 6. For large firms

You already have systems: accounting, job software, your own email server.
We don't replace them. We connect to them, so the AI agents work with your real data and your accountant's figures.
Every firm is different, so we look first, then quote. No fixed price.

## 7. Start small: the pilot

We are taking 3 firms only. They help shape the product.
£100 for 3 months, a short call every two weeks, and a lower price if they stay.
After 3 months you'll know if it saves you time. If not, you walk away, and we give you all your data back.

## 8. Close

Your emails, your jobs and the rules, in one place you can talk to.
Checked, cited and private.
Who wants to be one of the first three?
