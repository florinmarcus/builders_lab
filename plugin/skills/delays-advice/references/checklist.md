# Delay checklist

- **Deadline:** the target or completion date and days left (`get_job_timeline`).
- **Cause:** who caused the delay, with the email that proves it (e.g. the client's or council's own contractor moved a date).
- **Notice duty:** the clause that says notify, in writing, within N days, to whom. Count from the date we became aware.
- **Extension grounds:** the clause that allows more time, and whether the cause fits it.
- **Charges:** liquidated damages per day, and when they start.
- **Already sent:** our last emails on the job (timeline `email_sent`). Did they warn of the risk?
- **Who:** the contract manager or named person, with email.
- **Protect yourself:** notice in time, the reason, a revised date, keep records (photos, emails), ask for the extension in writing.
- **Gaps:** what the contract does not say; who to ask (the contract manager, an adviser).
