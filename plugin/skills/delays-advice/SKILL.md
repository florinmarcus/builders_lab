# Delays advice

Read `../../shared/rules.md`. Not legal advice: say so once, briefly.

1. `get_job_timeline` for the job: target date, days left, what we sent and received (with dates), the reason for the delay.
2. The contract: `get_job` documents and `search_job_documents` (delay, notify, extension, target date, damages, contract manager). Quote clauses with numbers. If silent, search the rules with the `regs-search-plan` skill; if nothing, say so.
3. Answer with `references/checklist.md`, in this order: should you send, how and by when, how to protect yourself, who to contact (email), possible charges, what you already sent.
4. Offer the notice as a draft in English, addressed to the contract manager, citing the clauses and the cause, with a revised date; offer a calendar reminder for the notice deadline. Never send.
